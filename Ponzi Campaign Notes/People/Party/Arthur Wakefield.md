# Arthur Wakefield

![[a02-arthur-student-id.jpg|200]]

> [!abstract] 👤 Person
> A "real student" with occult leanings who shared Ponzi's hunger for [[The Black]]. Found dead at home shortly after joining the Bureau.
> **Status:** Dead (I-09)

## What we know
- Finds many empty vials at the Black party; throws a "grenade" at the deal. ([[I-01 Mystery of the Ponzi Potion|I-01]])
- Shows up with an unknown person; arrested for aggravated assault, let off with public disturbance and a $50 fine due 31 May. ([[I-02 The Stakeout and the Case against Arthur Wakefield|I-02]])
- Found bloody on the riverbank, "out of it". ([[I-03 Trouble in Texaxe|I-03]])
- Retrieves [[Dover's Book]] from the hospital and lies about it; admits contact with the occult — "It's all devilwork." ([[I-04 TJs and Js - Suspects of Devilwork|I-04]])
- Ponzi's breakdown scrawl: "HE DID IT — ARTHUR MUST DIE". ([[I-05 Cliffs before Dover|I-05]])
- Left "more or less dead" outside the barn. ([[I-06 The Cliffs of Dover|I-06]])
- Understands "the power" of the Black like Ponzi does; could *feel* the old man's drawings; had a method of contacting [[Jimmy]]. ([[I-08 It's Just Jimmy|I-08]])
- **Found dead at home** — possibly from the Black. ([[I-09 Eggventures of Chuckleberry Jim|I-09]])
- [[Arthur's Notes]] trigger Ponzi's comet vision. ([[I-12 The Liar, the Snitch and the Wardrobe|I-12]])
- His unfinished task was in [[Innsmouth]]. ([[I-23 In Insmuth|I-23]])
- [[Arthur's Belongings]]: leather jacket, metal playing cards, empty vials, a vial of dark goop, a vial of blood. ([[II-06 Chief Luther and Long-Armed John|II-06]])
