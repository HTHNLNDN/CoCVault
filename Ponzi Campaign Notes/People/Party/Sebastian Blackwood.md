# Sebastian Blackwood

> [!abstract] 👤 Person
> Rich young heir of the [[Blackwood Estate]], bookish, Latin-reading, and greedier than he looks. [[Jimmy]]'s replacement for Arthur; by July 1924 a "higher rank".
> **Status:** Alive — Bureau agent

## What we know
- Introduced by [[Jimmy]] as Arthur's replacement — "rich young guy… not self made". ([[I-10 A Slim Chance of Shady|I-10]])
- "Spent his time on the books"; had a whole estate just for the job. His chant ends [[The Apartment Paradox]]. ([[I-11 Apartmentdox|I-11]])
- Points a gun at Ponzi; Ponzi thinks him "young, but greedy". ([[I-18 Muddy Thoughts|I-18]])
- Tries to take the skulls. ([[I-21 Skulls 4 the Skull Throne|I-21]])
- Takes everyone to the main [[Blackwood Estate]]. ([[I-22 Dog Water|I-22]])
- Finds the [[Innsmouth Gold Forge]]. ([[I-24 The Last Straw|I-24]])
- [[Jeremiah]] gives Ponzi a letter meant for him. ([[I-25 Dreamers Abound|I-25]])
- Reads the Latin of the [[Bible of Father Felix]]. ([[II-05 Father Felix|II-05]])
- Knows "an eccentric" into sacrifice; keeps [[Ghouls (The Dogs)|the dogs]] in his basement; starts a business venture. ([[II-07 The Bible and the Everliving Flame|II-07]])
- Meets [[Dallas]] at a socialite party; plants a story in the Austin Statesman. ([[II-08 The Framing of Elias|II-08]])
- Holds up [[The Elias Mask]]. ([[II-09 Mortimer, the Mask and the Chapters|II-09]])
- Spends his Bureau voucher on info about "the Dogs"; plans to settle them under [[Gloucester]]. ([[II-10 John, Shelly and Gloucester|II-10]])
- One of his staff burns up in the purification. ([[II-11 The Purification|II-11]])
- A professor knows him; he finds a way to control an eldritch being. ([[II-12 The Forbidden Library|II-12]])
- Verse: grandpa planned his days, his **sister "went to the moon"**, Jimmy sent him to Innsmouth with one coin; he became "the Marsh heir". ([[II-13 Fresh Fish of Marsh Heir|II-13]])
