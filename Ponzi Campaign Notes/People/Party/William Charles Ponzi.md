# William Charles Ponzi

![[a20-ponzi-photo.jpg|200]] ![[a20-ponzi-character-sheet.jpg|520]]

> [!abstract] 👤 Person
> Conman from Springfield, Alabama (39). Narrator of Notebook I. Accused of selling [[The Black]], recruited into [[The Bureau]], slowly consumed by the Black, [[The Sigils|the sigils]] and [[The Jewelled Skull|skulls]] — and finally a **deserter**.
> **Status:** Deserter from the Bureau — whereabouts unknown; appears to the Notebook II narrator at night

## What we know
- Accused of selling [[The Black]]; someone is impersonating him from [[The Slaughterhouse]]. ([[I-01 Mystery of the Ponzi Potion|I-01]])
- Years ago he sold a useless drug to [[Jacob Dover]]'s dying mother — the root of Dover's vendetta. ([[I-01 Mystery of the Ponzi Potion|I-01]])
- Husband of [[Martha Ponzi|Martha]], with one child. Writes home about a tonic that lets him "peer into the future". ([[I-07 The Cliffs are Dover|I-07]])
- Steals the Black from [[TJ]] — "I have to have it". Later trades 3 vials to [[Vince (The Spider)|the Spider]]. ([[I-08 It's Just Jimmy|I-08]])
- Brags of selling "fairy-drink" to Gatsby; grew up among Alabama maize mazes. ([[I-11 Apartmentdox|I-11]])
- Reads [[Arthur's Notes]], relives a vision of Halley's comet, falls — and wakes up knowing [[The Sigils]]. ([[I-12 The Liar, the Snitch and the Wardrobe|I-12]])
- Pulls away from [[TJ]] and [[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]] (they point guns at him); starts collecting skulls; Martha sees **tattoos on his head** — "I knew I was chosen". ([[I-18 Muddy Thoughts|I-18]])
- Completes [[The Jewelled Skull]], wakes among [[Ghouls (The Dogs)|ghouls]]; an old scam as **"Eduardo"** (vacation homes, down South) catches up with him. ([[I-21 Skulls 4 the Skull Throne|I-21]])
- Hides the real skulls, smashes two replicas and convinces [[Jimmy]] they are destroyed. ([[I-22 Dog Water|I-22]])
- Meets [[G-Man]] in [[Innsmouth]], who tells him to get out; he plans to go back to Alabama. ([[I-24 The Last Straw|I-24]])
- [[Gates]] declares him a **deserter**; his house is abandoned, a rotting woman in the bedroom and a Black ritual in the kitchen. ([[II-04 The Search for Ponzi|II-04]])
- Appears to the [[Notebook II Narrator|Notebook II narrator]] at night: cleanse his wife & child with [[Purifying Flame]], and he will reveal the Black Forest beast. Refuses to speak with TJ & Sebastian. ([[II-07 The Bible and the Everliving Flame|II-07]])
- Leaves a message on the narrator's bed. ([[II-10 John, Shelly and Gloucester|II-10]])
- His wife & child arrive on two Nile crocodiles and are purified; he names [[Blackwood Forest]] as the beast's lair. ([[II-11 The Purification|II-11]])
