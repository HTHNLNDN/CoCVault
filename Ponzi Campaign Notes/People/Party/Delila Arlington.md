# Delila Arlington

![[a01-delila-darling-portrait.jpg|200]] ![[a03-delila-never-speak-of-this.jpg|200]]

> [!abstract] 👤 Person
> Silent-movie star with connections everywhere — nightlife, police, and "lots of secret peoples". Also known (in Ponzi's mock-Cyrillic) as **"The Shiv", Comrade Extraordinaire**.
> **Status:** Alive — last seen at the mansion (I-11)

## What we know
- Introduced as "Darling", a movie star who brings news about [[Dallas]] and seems to work with the police. ([[I-01 Mystery of the Ponzi Potion|I-01]])
- Traces [[The Black]] to [[The University (Arkham)|the university]] and the party scene. ([[I-01 Mystery of the Ponzi Potion|I-01]])
- "Listen Kiddo, I owe 1 and you owe one now. NEVER SPEAK OF THIS." ([[I-03 Trouble in Texaxe|I-03]])
- Runs off after the book fight. ([[I-04 TJs and Js - Suspects of Devilwork|I-04]])
- "Stared into the void horse, and it stared back." A hidden "ARLINGTON" section is pasted over. ([[I-05 Cliffs before Dover|I-05]])
- Knows of "lots of secret peoples"; Ponzi comes to with his fist on her; she shoots alongside TJ at the slaughterhouse. ([[I-06 The Cliffs of Dover|I-06]])
- Returns when the team moves into the mansion. ([[I-10 A Slim Chance of Shady|I-10]])
- Finds the room with [[The Spinning Coin]]. ([[I-11 Apartmentdox|I-11]])
