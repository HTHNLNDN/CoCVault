# TJ

![[a03-secret-tj-bush.jpg|180]] ![[a04-tj-lamppost.jpg|180]]

> [!abstract] 👤 Person
> Texan cowboy and crack shot, with the mob on his tail and a weakness for terrible disguises. With the party from the first night; by July 1924 a "higher rank" in [[The Bureau]].
> **Status:** Alive — Bureau agent

## What we know
- Bursts into Ponzi's deal with [[Dallas]] alongside an officer ("TJ & GOP"), then chases Dallas. ([[I-01 Mystery of the Ponzi Potion|I-01]])
- [[Long-Armed John]] comes asking for "Thomson (TJ?)". ([[I-02 The Stakeout and the Case against Arthur Wakefield|I-02]])
- [[The Chapters (Mob)|The mob]] is after him — note from a Main Street hotel. Disguises himself as a bush. ([[I-03 Trouble in Texaxe|I-03]])
- Furious that [[Arthur Wakefield|Arthur]] brought [[Dover's Book]] home; kicks him out. Hides as a lamppost. ([[I-04 TJs and Js - Suspects of Devilwork|I-04]])
- Shoots [[Long-Armed John]] through a scope. Believes the Dreamer realm is related to God. ([[I-05 Cliffs before Dover|I-05]])
- Gets **cursed**; later kills [[Jacob Dover]]. ([[I-06 The Cliffs of Dover|I-06]])
- Temporarily blinded during the Bureau exam; hands Ponzi [[Arthur's Notes]]. ([[I-12 The Liar, the Snitch and the Wardrobe|I-12]])
- [[The Lake Blob]] targets him above everyone else. ([[I-19 Prismatic Shards|I-19]])
- Cuffs Ponzi and brings him back. ([[I-20 Fog of War|I-20]])
- Queasy after the Innsmouth factory — "saw some old colleagues of his". ([[I-24 The Last Straw|I-24]])
- Described as goodhearted, slightly foolish, "empty behind the eyes" but a mighty fine shot. ([[II-01 The Meeting|II-01]])
- Dreams of the prairie. ([[II-03 Little Bear and the Shirt|II-03]])
- Shoots [[Father Felix Oakley]], who explodes into wax. Has a contact in Houston ([[Maxwell Gibson]]) and is a regular at a gun shop. ([[II-05 Father Felix|II-05]])
- "TJ's compass" is fed to locate [[Long-Armed John]]. ([[II-06 Chief Luther and Long-Armed John|II-06]])
- Fond of [[Only Betsy]] in Austin. ([[II-07 The Bible and the Everliving Flame|II-07]])
- His friend [[Elias]] is framed as head of Chapter 3; [[Peterson]] is an old colleague; a society lady wants to "get her hands on" him. ([[II-08 The Framing of Elias|II-08]])
- Bounty hunters come for him at the warehouse. ([[II-09 Mortimer, the Mask and the Chapters|II-09]])
