# Notebook II Narrator

> [!abstract] 👤 Person
> The investigator who writes Notebook II (from 4 July 1924): a big-game hunter recruited by [[Gates]], partnered with [[TJ]] and [[Sebastian Blackwood|Blackwood]]. Carries an [[Elephant Gun]] and a cursed [[The Shirt|shirt/armor]], and wants public glory for "something grand".
> **Status:** Alive — Bureau recruit

## What we know
- Signs the Bureau contract but privately intends to go public with his success. ([[II-01 The Meeting|II-01]])
- Plans reinforced pit-traps and cage traps; builds a barbed-wire snare. ([[II-01 The Meeting|II-01]])
- His mind "contracts" and repels whatever tried to seize him in the hanging-hunters clearing. ([[II-02 The Jersey Devil|II-02]])
- Must commune with the spirits of [[The Shirt]] alone on [[The Prairie]]. ([[II-03 Little Bear and the Shirt|II-03]])
- Tries to mercy-kill the rotting woman in Ponzi's house. ([[II-04 The Search for Ponzi|II-04]])
- Gags [[Father Felix Oakley]] with gauze. ([[II-05 Father Felix|II-05]])
- Wants a ghoul as a trophy; gets his [[Elephant Gun]] fire-imbued with the [[Sigil Ring]]. Ponzi visits him at night. ([[II-07 The Bible and the Everliving Flame|II-07]])
- Refused entry at [[The Tavern]] (taken for a cop); beats [[Laurie]] in a fistfight. Dreams of shadowy men on the prairie. ([[II-08 The Framing of Elias|II-08]])
- Performs the [[Purifying Flame]] for Ponzi's family. ([[II-11 The Purification|II-11]])
- Finds the [[German Spellbook]] on messages of the Dreamer. ([[II-12 The Forbidden Library|II-12]])
