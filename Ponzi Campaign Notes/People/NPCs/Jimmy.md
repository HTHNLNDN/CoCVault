# Jimmy

![[a06-grand-wizard-jimmy.jpg|200]]

> [!abstract] 👤 Person
> The party's handler in [[The Bureau]] (department Prometheus). Pragmatic to the point of coldness; runs the wardrobe exam and keeps a vault.
> **Status:** Alive

## What we know
- Receives the party from [[B. Fisher]]. ([[I-07 The Cliffs are Dover|I-07]])
- From "the Bureau", department **Prometheus**, which collects records on strange beings. ([[I-08 It's Just Jimmy|I-08]])
- Gives the first task (stolen Greek text & coins); Arthur had a way to contact him. ([[I-09 Eggventures of Chuckleberry Jim|I-09]])
- Asks about Arthur's death only "as far as necessary for business"; recruits [[Sebastian Blackwood]]; moves the team to the mansion. ([[I-10 A Slim Chance of Shady|I-10]])
- Pulls the team from Curzon St — "lairs of snakes". ([[I-11 Apartmentdox|I-11]])
- Won't name the organisation — "there is power in a name"; runs the [[The Magic Wardrobe|wardrobe exam]]. ([[I-12 The Liar, the Snitch and the Wardrobe|I-12]])
- Sends the team South. ([[I-13 The Sigils|I-13]])
- Ponzi believes Jimmy supports his experimenting. ([[I-18 Muddy Thoughts|I-18]])
- Closes the wardrobe connection on Ponzi. ([[I-20 Fog of War|I-20]])
- Believes the skulls were destroyed. ([[I-22 Dog Water|I-22]])
- Not much help about Ponzi. ([[II-08 The Framing of Elias|II-08]])
- Keeps cells in [[Jimmy's Vault]]. ([[II-08 The Framing of Elias|II-08]])
- Sent Sebastian to Innsmouth with "one little coin". ([[II-13 Fresh Fish of Marsh Heir|II-13]])
