# G-Man

![[a17-g-man-office.jpg|200]]

> [!abstract] 👁️ Entity
> A man "clearly of Egyptian descent" with **many names**, met in a beautiful office Ponzi was suddenly transported to. Advised Ponzi to leave Innsmouth. [[Jeremiah's Letter]] is addressed to him.

## What we know
- Appears when Ponzi runs to the falling TJ; claims many names; advice: get out of [[Innsmouth]]. ([[I-24 The Last Straw|I-24]])
- Recipient of Jeremiah's letter: "I've fled… Let's get working on this debt." ([[I-25 Dreamers Abound|I-25]])

## Connections
- [[Jeremiah]]
- [[William Charles Ponzi]]

> [!tip] Mythos lore (speculation)
> In Lovecraft's stories, a dark "Egyptian" man with many names is the classic guise of **Nyarlathotep**. Nothing in the journal confirms it.
