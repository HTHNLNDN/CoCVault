# Dallas

> [!abstract] 👤 Person
> Texas gangster of [[The Chapters (Mob)|the mob]]. Wanted to deal with Ponzi, escaped the chase, later fought beside the party against Dover — and in July 1924 ended up in [[Jimmy's Vault]] telling everything about the Chapters.
> **Status:** Alive — released via B. Fisher (II-09)

## What we know
- Eager to strike a deal with Ponzi; escapes after TJ and the police storm in; wins the car chase. ([[I-01 Mystery of the Ponzi Potion|I-01]])
- Backs the party up in the assault on [[The Slaughterhouse]]. ([[I-06 The Cliffs of Dover|I-06]])
- At a socialite party, pulls [[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]] into a meeting; has a contact at a local bank. ([[II-08 The Framing of Elias|II-08]])
- Held at the [[Blackwood Estate]]; bribes staff to pass a message; says a car from [[Saltonstall]] will collect him; locked in [[Jimmy's Vault]]. ([[II-08 The Framing of Elias|II-08]])
- Interrogated (good cop/bad cop): lays out [[The Chapters (Mob)|the Chapters]]; says "J" = [[Long-Armed John]], "N" = [[The Never Man]]. Released via [[B. Fisher]]. ([[II-09 Mortimer, the Mask and the Chapters|II-09]])
