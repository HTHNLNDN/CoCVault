# Jacob Dover

> [!abstract] 👤 Person
> Owner of [[The Slaughterhouse]] with a vendetta against Ponzi, who once sold his dying mother a useless cure. Wielded demonic powers; killed by TJ mid-ritual.
> **Status:** Dead (I-06)

## What we know
- Bought the abandoned slaughterhouse at 143 River Street; [[Dover's Book]] under his bed tells of his mother's lung disease and Ponzi's fake cure. ([[I-01 Mystery of the Ponzi Potion|I-01]])
- Seems to have learned Ponzi's name somehow — maybe via Arthur's stranger. ([[I-02 The Stakeout and the Case against Arthur Wakefield|I-02]])
- His father has strange ramblings; none of the stories are local. ([[I-03 Trouble in Texaxe|I-03]])
- Waits for Arthur: bring TJ to the slaughterhouse. ([[I-04 TJs and Js - Suspects of Devilwork|I-04]])
- Summons demons. ([[I-05 Cliffs before Dover|I-05]])
- Moonless ritual, drawing sigils — killed by [[TJ]]. ([[I-06 The Cliffs of Dover|I-06]])
- His house searched again — dead end. ([[I-09 Eggventures of Chuckleberry Jim|I-09]])
