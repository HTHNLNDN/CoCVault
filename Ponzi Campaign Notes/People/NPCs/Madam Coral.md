# Madam Coral

> [!abstract] 👤 Person
> Stout little shopkeeper in [[Innsmouth]] who only trades for things or information, and clearly has an agenda. She once spoke of an **8th gate** (name hard to read in the journal — "Madam Corel").

## What we know
- Sells Ponzi a hat and "finger-licking" cigarettes; warns of car trouble — then the car is sabotaged. ([[I-23 In Insmuth|I-23]])
- The team tells her what they knew before leaving Innsmouth. ([[I-24 The Last Straw|I-24]])
- Sebastian's verse: "went to a shop, and the car broke in a suspicious way". ([[II-13 Fresh Fish of Marsh Heir|II-13]])
- The narrator recalls she spoke of an 8th gate. ([[II-15 The Gates and the Children|II-15]])
- **She is the Innsmouth shopkeeper.** (player note, 2 Oct 2026)
