# Chief Luther

![[a23-chief-luther-chalk-outline.jpg|200]]

> [!abstract] 👤 Person
> Police chief the party reported to in Arkham. Knew more about [[B. Fisher]] than he let on, and was told by [[Gates]] to bury the Longarm evidence. Shot dead in the street in July 1924.
> **Status:** Dead (II-06)

## What we know
- Not pleased with what the party reports. ([[I-01 Mystery of the Ponzi Potion|I-01]])
- Has Arthur brought in and arrested. ([[I-02 The Stakeout and the Case against Arthur Wakefield|I-02]])
- Tries to hide that he knows [[B. Fisher]]. ([[I-03 Trouble in Texaxe|I-03]])
- Killed on the street — "a good shot", says the new chief. [[Gates]] had directed him to bury the evidence in the [[Long-Armed John|Longarm]] case. ([[II-06 Chief Luther and Long-Armed John|II-06]])
