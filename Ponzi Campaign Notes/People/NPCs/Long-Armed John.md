# Long-Armed John

![[a04-long-armed-john-scope.jpg|200]] ![[b05-bag-of-john.jpg|200]]

> [!abstract] 👤 Person
> Mob enforcer who hunted [[TJ]]. Shot by TJ in Arkham — then his fungus-covered body vanished from the morgue. Tracked to Boston, bagged, and handed to [[Shelly]] for study.
> **Status:** In Bureau custody (II-12) — dead? re-animated?

## What we know
- Visits Arthur in his cell looking for "Thomson (TJ?)". ([[I-02 The Stakeout and the Case against Arthur Wakefield|I-02]])
- Waits at the safehouse door; tells TJ to meet at [[Independence Square]]; dodges Arthur. ([[I-04 TJs and Js - Suspects of Devilwork|I-04]])
- Shot by TJ through a scope. ([[I-05 Cliffs before Dover|I-05]])
- Autopsy: covered in slimy fungal growth; his body vanished from the morgue. [[Gates]] had Luther bury the evidence. "Maybe John just walked off himself." ([[II-06 Chief Luther and Long-Armed John|II-06]])
- Located in [[Boston]] with [[The Compass]]; ambushed, sacked and carried off; "John on the roof… Dead." ([[II-06 Chief Luther and Long-Armed John|II-06]])
- Dallas claims he is the "J" of the letter. ([[II-09 Mortimer, the Mask and the Chapters|II-09]])
- Seeping through the bag, sprouting spores and flowers; traded to [[Shelly]]. ([[II-10 John, Shelly and Gloucester|II-10]])
- Strapped to Shelly's table, head split open with probes — but **Mr. J ≠ John**. ([[II-12 The Forbidden Library|II-12]])
