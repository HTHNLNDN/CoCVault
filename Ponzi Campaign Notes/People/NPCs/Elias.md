# Elias

> [!abstract] 👤 Person
> TJ's friend, framed and jailed as the "head honcho" of Chapter 3 on faked photographic evidence, and found not guilty — someone wore [[The Elias Mask|a mask of his face]].
> **Status:** Acquitted — found not guilty

## What we know
- Accused via photos; remembers none of it. The photos contain a paper dated 4 June, though supposedly taken 25 April. ([[II-08 The Framing of Elias|II-08]])
- Alibi for 22 Jan, weaker one for 9 Jan; a corpse with an Elias mask is found; nearly enough to acquit. ([[II-09 Mortimer, the Mask and the Chapters|II-09]])
- **Found not guilty.** (player note, 2 Oct 2026)
