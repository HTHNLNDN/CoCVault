# Martha Ponzi

> [!abstract] 👤 Person
> Ponzi's wife in Alabama, mother of his child. Probably the woman found rotting in the mold of [[Ponzi's House (Alabama)|Ponzi's house]] — later purified with her child.
> **Status:** Purified (II-11) — condition unknown

## What we know
- Ponzi writes her about a future-seeing tonic. ([[I-07 The Cliffs are Dover|I-07]])
- Tells Ponzi he has tattoos on his head. ([[I-18 Muddy Thoughts|I-18]])
- Ponzi sees her in the fog; then she's gone. ([[I-20 Fog of War|I-20]])
- A woman rots in the moldy bedroom and won't die even with her throat cut (identity not stated). ([[II-04 The Search for Ponzi|II-04]])
- Ponzi asks for his wife & child to be cleansed. ([[II-07 The Bible and the Everliving Flame|II-07]])
- Delivered on a Nile crocodile and purged by sunlight. ([[II-11 The Purification|II-11]])
