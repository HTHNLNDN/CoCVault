# Gates

> [!abstract] 👤 Person
> **L. Colonel A. J. Gates** — senior Bureau officer. Recruits the Notebook II narrator, orders the hunt for Ponzi, and is the addressee of the Operation Last Supper memo.

## What we know
- Addressee of [[Handout - Top Secret Archival Entry 407]] (access level: First Lieutenant). ([[I-22 Dog Water|I-22]])
- Leads the narrator into a strange room to sign the Bureau contract. ([[II-01 The Meeting|II-01]])
- Orders the team to find Ponzi before the Bureau learns he deserted; then decides "nothing more to do". ([[II-04 The Search for Ponzi|II-04]])
- Says re-animation is "not unusual"; had Luther bury the evidence in the Longarm case. ([[II-06 Chief Luther and Long-Armed John|II-06]])
