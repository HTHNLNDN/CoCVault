# The University (Arkham)

![[b06-forbidden-library.jpg|200]]

> [!abstract] 📍 Place
> The university in Arkham (the journal never names it — presumably Miskatonic). Centre of the Black trade; its library holds a restricted/forbidden section.

## What we know
- The Black is centred here; seller route on the medical campus. ([[I-01 Mystery of the Ponzi Potion|I-01]])
- Ponzi searches the library about Dover's father. ([[I-03 Trouble in Texaxe|I-03]])
- Ponzi sees Dr. Chase here. ([[I-08 It's Just Jimmy|I-08]])
- Vince "the Spider" hangs around. ([[I-10 A Slim Chance of Shady|I-10]])
- A professor who knows Sebastian opens the restricted library; the forbidden section is next. ([[II-12 The Forbidden Library|II-12]])
- [[German Spellbook]] found in the library. ([[II-12 The Forbidden Library|II-12]])
