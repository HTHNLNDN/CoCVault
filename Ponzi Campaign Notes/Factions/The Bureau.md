# The Bureau

> [!abstract] 🏛️ Faction
> Nameless secret organisation that handles the occult ("there is power in a name"). The party's employer from I-07 onward.

## What we know
- [[B. Fisher]] hands the party to [[Jimmy]]. ([[I-07 The Cliffs are Dover|I-07]])
- Department **Prometheus** collects records on strange beings. ([[I-08 It's Just Jimmy|I-08]])
- Codenames in the field (John Smith, "Fisher"); exam via [[The Magic Wardrobe]]. ([[I-12 The Liar, the Snitch and the Wardrobe|I-12]])
- Secrecy contract. ([[II-01 The Meeting|II-01]])
- Departments: **Prometheus** (researchers), **Daedalus** (containment), **Astraea** (internal police). Medusa protocol for section C; deserters are picked up. ([[II-02 The Jersey Devil|II-02]])
- Ponzi is a deserter. ([[II-04 The Search for Ponzi|II-04]])
- Trades information for specimens and blood. ([[II-10 John, Shelly and Gloucester|II-10]])
