# The Chapters (Mob)

> [!abstract] 🏛️ Faction
> Organised crime network split into numbered "Chapters", each with its own city or speciality. Hunts TJ; runs the Black and Innsmouth gold.

## What we know
- The mob is after TJ. ([[I-03 Trouble in Texaxe|I-03]])
- Mob-linked Maxwell Gibson dead; "J" letter. ([[II-05 Father Felix|II-05]])
- Elias framed as head of Chapter 3. ([[II-08 The Framing of Elias|II-08]])
- Dallas maps the chapters. ([[II-09 Mortimer, the Mask and the Chapters|II-09]])
