# Esoteric Order of Dagon

> [!abstract] 🏛️ Faction
> The cult Cthulhu created while sleeping: an order of [[Deep Ones]], led by **Dagon and Mother Hydra**, the oldest, "born from his dreams".

## What we know
- Created by Cthulhu while sleeping; an order of Deep Ones; led by Dagon & Mother Hydra. ([[II-16 The Cult of Cthulhu|II-16]])
- Many under-water cities pray to Dagon. ([[II-17 The Prophecy of the Stones|II-17]])
