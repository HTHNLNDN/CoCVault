# Bible of Father Felix

![[a22-bible-of-father-felix.jpg|200]] ![[b02-saint-luke-illumination.jpg|200]]

> [!abstract] 🗝️ Item
> Father Felix Oakley's annotated Latin book (a 1162 manuscript by Brother Adalbert). Holds his notes on becoming a wax vessel, enchantments and prayers.

## What we know
- Taken from the church; Sebastian finds circled passages on light and "a congregation to contain the angel". ([[II-05 Father Felix|II-05]])
- Yields instructions to consume an angel and three spells. ([[II-07 The Bible and the Everliving Flame|II-07]])
