# The Everliving Flame

![[b01-everliving-flame.jpg|200]]

> [!abstract] 🗝️ Item
> The flame that never goes out, from Father Felix's basement. Only a human can carry it. Now in the Blackwood drawing-room hearth. Using it requires sacrifice.

## What we know
- Kept in Felix's basement. ([[II-04 The Search for Ponzi|II-04]])
- Wax taken off the fire. ([[II-05 Father Felix|II-05]])
- Moved to the Blackwood Estate hearth; "using the fire requires sacrifice". ([[II-07 The Bible and the Everliving Flame|II-07]])
