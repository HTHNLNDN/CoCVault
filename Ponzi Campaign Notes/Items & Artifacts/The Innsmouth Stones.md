# The Innsmouth Stones

> [!abstract] 🗝️ Item
> Inscribed stones from Innsmouth ("the dreamer stones"). The narrator studies pictures of them: several are numbered **gates** with inscriptions, and their text reads as a prophecy of Cthulhu rising.

## What we know
- Pictures of the "dreamer stones"; the 10th and 5th gate inscriptions. ([[II-15 The Gates and the Children|II-15]])
- Translated: "Cthulhu will rise. When the distant lights align, stand ready to stop the fragments, the window becomes the door, they will rise from their inactivity." ([[II-17 The Prophecy of the Stones|II-17]])
