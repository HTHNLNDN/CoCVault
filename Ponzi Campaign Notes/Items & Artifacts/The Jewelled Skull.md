# The Jewelled Skull

![[a14-jewelled-skull.jpg|200]]

> [!abstract] 🗝️ Item
> Skull(s) Ponzi collected and "completed" with gems at an altar in Corpus Christi. The skull speaks to him. Ghouls and Sebastian want them; Ponzi hid the real ones.
> **Status:** Real skulls hidden by Ponzi (I-22)

## What we know
- "I have one"; needs a new skull for the island. ([[I-18 Muddy Thoughts|I-18]])
- Taken to the Corpus Christi altar with [[The Flute]]; it asks for jewels. ([[I-19 Prismatic Shards|I-19]])
- "It told me how to do it." ([[I-20 Fog of War|I-20]])
- Completed; it predicted Sebastian would try to take them. ([[I-21 Skulls 4 the Skull Throne|I-21]])
- Real ones hidden; two replicas smashed. ([[I-22 Dog Water|I-22]])
