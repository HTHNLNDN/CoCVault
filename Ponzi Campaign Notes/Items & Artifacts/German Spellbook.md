# German Spellbook

> [!abstract] 🗝️ Item
> Found in the university library: information on interpreting messages of the Dreamer.

## What we know
- Found by the narrator. ([[II-12 The Forbidden Library|II-12]])
- Probably the *Gospels of the Dreamer*: disciples see visions and do the Dreamer's bidding; the dreams reach mostly creative people, in outbreaks tied to a place. Spells learned: [[The Voorish Sign]], [[Ritual Dagger Enchantment]]. ([[II-14 The Gospels of the Dreamer|II-14]])
- Reading about the Children ([[Nug and Yeb]]) turns up [[Banishment of Y'detat]]. ([[II-15 The Gates and the Children|II-15]])
- Reveals the Dreamer as [[Cthulhu]]; spell [[Send Dream]] obtained. ([[II-16 The Cult of Cthulhu|II-16]])
