# Sanctified Wax

> [!abstract] 🗝️ Item
> Altar-candle wax Father Felix bathed in to become a vessel; he exploded into it when shot. Required for every enchantment.

## What we know
- Felix explodes into wax; the team collects it. ([[II-05 Father Felix|II-05]])
- All enchantments require the wax (or a substitute) and the ethereal flame. ([[II-07 The Bible and the Everliving Flame|II-07]])
