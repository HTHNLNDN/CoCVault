# The Black

![[a01-vial-of-black.jpg|200]]

> [!abstract] 🗝️ Item
> Black, milky drug. Taken raw it kills (suffocation, black tongue); diluted in alcohol it gives visions "of the future". Ponzi was blamed for selling it — then got hooked.

## What we know
- Causes social deterioration and insanity; killed Rodrick and two students; sold by Paul Rodgers around the university. ([[I-01 Mystery of the Ponzi Potion|I-01]])
- Ponzi describes it to Martha as a tonic to see the future. ([[I-07 The Cliffs are Dover|I-07]])
- Ponzi steals TJ's supply; Arthur also craves it. ([[I-08 It's Just Jimmy|I-08]])
- Arthur dead, maybe from it; Dr. Chase saw how people die of it. ([[I-09 Eggventures of Chuckleberry Jim|I-09]])
- 3 vials paid to the Spider. ([[I-10 A Slim Chance of Shady|I-10]])
- Handout: residue collected; refuses to mix with water, vanishes when water is poured on it; alcohol dilutes it into a vision drug. ([[I-14 Corpus Christi TX|I-14]])
- A ritual relating to "BLACK" in Ponzi's kitchen. ([[II-04 The Search for Ponzi|II-04]])
- Arthur's things include a vial of dark goop. ([[II-06 Chief Luther and Long-Armed John|II-06]])
