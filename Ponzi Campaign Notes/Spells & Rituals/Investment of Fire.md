# Investment of Fire

> [!abstract] 🔥 Spell
> Enchant a container so everything inside is imbued with fire. Higher up-front cost = lower cost per use. Requires the wax and the ethereal flame.

## What we know
- Read in the Bible; used on the elephant gun via the sigil ring. ([[II-07 The Bible and the Everliving Flame|II-07]])
