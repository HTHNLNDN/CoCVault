# Purifying Flame

![[b05-purification-pentagram.jpg|260]]

> [!abstract] 🔥 Spell
> Father Felix's healing/cleansing prayer: burns corruption from flesh and soul. Needs a focus, 24 h sunrise-to-sunrise, a long chant, and helpers keeping frankincense-and-myrrh incense (bathed in the wax) burning. Violent; costs both target and caster.

- Found in the Bible — "what Ponzi wanted". ([[II-07 The Bible and the Everliving Flame|II-07]])
- Ingredients gathered; ritual postponed. ([[II-08 The Framing of Elias|II-08]])
- Cast on Ponzi's wife & child; a staff member burns. ([[II-11 The Purification|II-11]])
