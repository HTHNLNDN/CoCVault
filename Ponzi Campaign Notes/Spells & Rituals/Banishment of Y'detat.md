# Banishment of Y'detat

> [!abstract] 🔥 Spell
> Banishes **non-human intelligences**. Needs three people and time.

## What we know
- Found while reading about the Children: cost D4 SAN, D4 MP, 3 people + time. ([[II-15 The Gates and the Children|II-15]])

> [!tip] Rules note
> The name is hard to read in the scan. The Call of Cthulhu rulebook has a spell called **Banishment of Yde Etad**, which is probably the one.
