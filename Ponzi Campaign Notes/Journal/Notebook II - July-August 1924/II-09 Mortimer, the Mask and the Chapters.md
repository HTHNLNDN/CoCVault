# II-09 · Mortimer, the Mask and the Chapters (26–28 July 1924)

> [!summary] At a glance
> - New lawyer **[[Mortimer]]**: Elias has an alibi for 22 Jan, a shakier one for 9 Jan.
> - At [[The Ex-Mob Warehouse]] the elevator shaft is concreted over; inside, a weeks-old corpse with its face melted off — and the sloughed face is **[[The Elias Mask|a mask of Elias]]**.
> - Bounty hunters came for TJ; escape by car. [[Saltonstall]] sends police to the estate looking for [[Dallas]].
> - Dallas's interrogation maps **[[The Chapters (Mob)|the Chapters]]**; "J" in the letter = [[Long-Armed John]], "N" = [[The Never Man]].
> - Mortimer believes they almost have enough to acquit Eli. [[B. Fisher]] is called to release Dallas.

## Transcript
After we went to the office of [[Otis]] to speak with the new lawyer.

[[Mortimer]] said that [[Elias]] had an alibi ~~for~~ for the 22. of Jan. ~~Together we decided~~

He also had somewhat of an alibi for the 9th of Jan, but it wasn't bulletproof, so we decided to look for clues ~~in~~ at the ex-mob warehouse. → [[The Ex-Mob Warehouse]]

![[b04-mortimer.jpg|240]]
*Mortimer in his office.*

When we entered we looked around and found that the elevator shaft had been paved with concrete.

After breaking up the concrete we found a corpse, the face completely melted off. By the look of the corpse it seems to have been here for at least a couple of weeks.

[[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]] took the sloughed off face and held it up, sure enough looks like Elias. And upon further looking found that it was a mask. → [[The Elias Mask]]

When I looked upstairs I found some traces from some guys. Apparently some bounty hunters came to take[?] [[TJ]].

We escaped by driving the car up to the side of the warehouse and TJ jumping into the carriage.

Apparently [[Saltonstall]] sent police to the estate to look for [[Dallas]]. Of course they won't find anything.

![[b04-blackwood-estate.jpg|520]]
*The [[Blackwood Estate]]: a large manor with a tower, iron fence, pines and a statue.*

### Sunday 27-07-2[4] *(written "27-07-26")*
After a bit of kerfuffling about, we went to Dallas to ask questions. TJ & Sebastian decided to play good cop – bad cop.

From the conversation we gathered: → [[The Chapters (Mob)]]
- **Chapter 3** is distribution and operates out of Austin.
- **Chapter 2** are the "Beaters" & "Cleaners". They make space, solve problems.
- **Chapter 30**: Drug supply (just Arkham). → [[The Black]]?
- **Chapter 29**: Boston, politics.
- **Chapter 31**: Innsmouth Gold. → [[Innsmouth Gold Coins]], [[Innsmouth Gold Forge]]

Furthermore Sebastian & I went & reported to Mortimer, who believes we had almost enough to get Eli acquitted.

### Monday 28-07-2[4] *(written "28-07-26")*
We entered into the chambers of Dallas to continue[?] the talks.

From the telegram Dallas told us that the J of the letter is [[Long-Armed John]]. And N is [[The Never Man|the Never Man]][?]. → [[Mr. J]]

Furthermore:
- **Chapter 1**: Gets a lot of deliveries from Chapter 3.
- **Chapter 5**: Finances & Banking.
- **Chapter 10**: Gambling.
- **Chapter 15**: New Orleans, documents, IDs, permits etc.
- **Chapter 21**: DC, political influence.
- **Chapter 25**: Buying of higher people.
- **Chapter 8**: Blackmail

After which we decided to call for [[B. Fisher]] to release Dallas.
