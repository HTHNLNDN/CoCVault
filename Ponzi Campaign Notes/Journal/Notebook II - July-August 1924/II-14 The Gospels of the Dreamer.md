## Transcript
### Friday 01-08-24
In the morning [[Ghouls (The Dogs)|the dogs]] had left the estate. → [[Blackwood Estate]], [[Operation Last Supper and the Gloucester Dogs]]

I continued to read the Gospels of the dreamer ([[German Spellbook]]?). How the disciples saw visions of what the world could be, and that they were to do the Dreamer's bidding. It seemed to mostly be creatives who recieved the dreams, and it happened as location based outbreaks. → [[The Sleepers and the Dreamer]]

From this I learned the spells
- The Voorish sign: Amplify effects: 1 San + 1 MP → [[The Voorish Sign]]
- Ritual Dagger (enchant): Adds eldritch damage: Blood sacrifice + 5 POW → [[Ritual Dagger Enchantment]]

After which we go back to sleep.
