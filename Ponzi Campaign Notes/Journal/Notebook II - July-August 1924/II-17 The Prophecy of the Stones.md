### Monday 04-08-2[4] *(written "04-08-26")*
I spent the day translating the text on the stones of [[Innsmouth]]. It seems like a prophecy: → [[The Innsmouth Stones]]

> "[[Cthulhu]] will rise. When the distant lights align, stand ready to stop the fragments, the window becomes the door, they will rise from their inactivity".

So to stop it, we need to find what bound the Dreamers. There are many 'under-water' citys, that pray to Dagon. → [[The Prophecy of the Stones]], [[The Sleepers and the Dreamer]], [[Deep Ones]]
