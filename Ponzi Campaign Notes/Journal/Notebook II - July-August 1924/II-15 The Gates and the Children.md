# II-15 · The Gates and the Children (Sat 2 August 1924)

> [!summary] At a glance
> - Back at the library alone. **[[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]] was missing from the breakfast table.**
> - Studying pictures of the **dreamer stones** ([[The Innsmouth Stones]]), the narrator remembers **[[Madam Coral]]**[?] speaking of an **8th gate**. The 10th and 5th gates carry inscriptions. → [[The Gates on the Stones]]
> - **[[Nug and Yeb]]** are children of Yog-Sothoth, "dancing with each other in all eternity until the time is right". Nug → [[Cthulhu]]; Yeb → Sathshohau[?].
> - Spell found: **[[Banishment of Y'detat]]**[?] — banishes non-human intelligences.

## Transcript
### Saturday 02-08-24
In the morning I went back to the library, alone this time. [[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]] was not at the breakfast table. → [[The University (Arkham)]]

While studying the pictures of the dreamer stones I recalled that [[Madam Coral|Madam Corel]][?] spoke of an 8th gate. → [[The Innsmouth Stones]], [[The Gates on the Stones]]

The 10th Gate spoke:
> "R: Seek light, R: Birth, R: Skip Ahead, L: Gate"

The 5th Gate:
> "Nug & Yeb: Seek light, Birth, L:"

Nug & Yeb are children of Yog Sogoth. They represent life & creation, dancing with each other in all eternity until the time is right. → [[Nug and Yeb]]
- Yeb: Sathshohau[?]
- Nug: [[Cthulhu]]
- Children of the gods

While reading of the Children I also came upon a spell:
- Banishment of Y'detat[?]: Banish non-human intelligences: D4 San, D4 Magic and 3 people + time. → [[Banishment of Y'detat]]

I went back to sleep
