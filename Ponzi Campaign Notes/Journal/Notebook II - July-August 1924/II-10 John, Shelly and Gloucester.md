# II-10 · John, Shelly and Gloucester (Mon 28 July 1924)

> [!summary] At a glance
> - [[Long-Armed John|John's remains]] are seeping through the bag, sprouting spores and flowers.
> - At [[Shelly's Facility|Shelly's]], John is traded for information; the team **donates blood** for monster info. Sebastian spends his voucher on "the Dogs".
> - [[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]]'s plan: let [[Ghouls (The Dogs)|the Dogs]], led by **[[Anders (Ghoul)|'Anders']]**, infest the underground of **[[Gloucester]]** — a huge cave system with a mural wall and a sacrifice room.
> - Back home, [[William Charles Ponzi|Ponzi]] has left a message on the narrator's bed. [[B. Fisher]] is at the estate, annoyed.
