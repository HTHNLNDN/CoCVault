## Transcript
### Wednesday 30-07-24
After the ritual succeeded, we decided to go to [[The University (Arkham)|the university]] to ask about [[Innsmouth]].

A professor there, who knew [[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]], was very keen to talk. She was presented with some pictures, and took us to ~~the~~ the restricted library. We pressed her on getting access to the forbidden section, but she clearly has some apprehensions, apparently she had been consumed[?] before.

After talking of the vague prophesies she seemed to budge tho.

### Thursday 31-07-24 *(corrected from "Tuesday")*
![[b06-forbidden-library.jpg|220]]
*Forbidden library.*

We went to [[Shelly|Shelly's]], as he had news of [[Long-Armed John|John]]. In his lab, we found John strapped to an operating table. His head split open, with probes all over, rather gruesome.

John did not provide any answers we thought we ~~[struck]~~ wanted. Instead we found that **Mr. J ≠ John**... → [[Mr. J]]

Since that wasn't fruitful, we went to the university. In the library I found a German spellbook. The spellbook contained information on interpreting messages of the Dreamer. → [[German Spellbook]], [[The Sleepers and the Dreamer]]

Sebastian spent the day reading about some eldritch being, apparently he achieved a way of controlling.
