# II-04 · The Search for Ponzi (14–17 July 1924)

> [!summary] At a glance
> - [[Gates]] orders the team to find their former teammate **[[William Charles Ponzi|Ponzi]]** before the Bureau learns he has deserted.
> - [[Ponzi's House (Alabama)|Ponzi's house]] is abandoned; a letter asks them to take care of "her". In a moldy bedroom a woman rots in the dust and **does not die even when her throat is cut** ([[Martha Ponzi]]?). Corrupting dust covers house and farmland.
> - In the kitchen: remnants of a ritual related to "BLACK" → [[Bringing the Black Draught]], [[The Black]].
> - The letter's return address leads to a church in [[Austin]] run by a preacher visited by "an angel", with an undying flame and burnt bodies in the basement → [[Father Felix Oakley]].

## Transcript
### Monday – 14/7/1924
We were received by [[Gates]], who told us to find their earlier teammate [[William Charles Ponzi|Ponzi]], before [[The Bureau|the Bureau]] finds out he's a deserter.

We took the train to Alabama.

### Tuesday – 15/7/1924
We arrived in Alabama, and found [[Ponzi's House (Alabama)|Ponzi's house]]. The house had been abandoned but there was a letter left for us, asked us to take care of "her". We entered a room, that was full of mold, and in the bed was a woman who was rotting in the mold/dust. I tried to mercy kill her, but even after cutting the neck she did not die. → [[Martha Ponzi]]?

[[TJ]] observed that there was corrupting dust everywhere. The house, the farmland etc.

In the kitchen we found remnant of a ritual apparently relating to something called "BLACK". → [[Bringing the Black Draught]], [[The Black]]

![[a21-ponzi-kitchen-ritual.jpg|220]]
*The ritual remnants on the kitchen floor.*

We gave the known information to Gates, who decided there was nothing more to do.

### The Search for Ponzi — Tuesday 16-07-24
We ~~noticed~~ found out that the address on the letter from Ponzi was in Texas, [[Austin]].

We took the car the next day from Alabama to Austin, TX.

### Wednesday 17-07-24
Apparently it was the address of a church, with a rather colorful character who believed the world was supposed to end 5 years ago. He seemed a strange person, so we decided to investigate. → [[Father Felix's Church]]

The guy preached about having ~~seen~~ been visited by an angel. In his basement he kept a flame that wouldn't go out. There were also burnt bodies in the basement. Clearly people who did not pass his ritual blessing. → [[Father Felix Oakley]], [[The Angel]], [[The Everliving Flame]]

We wanted to bring him in but him having the abilities he does we decided to go find Gates.

He wasn't of much help, Flinn[?] didn't budge on showing the basement.
