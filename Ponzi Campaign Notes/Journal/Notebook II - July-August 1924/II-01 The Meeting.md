# II-01 · The Meeting (Fri 4 – Sun 6 July 1924)

> [!summary] At a glance
> - A new investigator is recruited by **[[Gates|Mr. Gates]]** into [[The Bureau]] and signs a secrecy contract (which he privately intends to break).
> - Partnered with two higher ranks: [[TJ]] ("goodhearted and slightly foolish", a fine shot) and [[Sebastian Blackwood|Blackwood]] ("silver spoon rich kid").
> - First job: a beast in New Jersey → [[The Jersey Devil]]. A barbed-wire snare trap catches nothing the first night.

← [[I-25 Dreamers Abound]] · Next → [[II-02 The Jersey Devil]]

## Transcript
### The Meeting | Friday 4/07/24
I was led into a strange room, by a [[Gates|Mr. Gates]]. There I signed the contract, promising not to publish in any way what I learn of. → [[The Bureau]]

This of course is preposterous, the public will know of my success, at least when I capture something grand enough. This has to be my life's work.

I'm meant to work with two higher ranks, [[TJ]] & [[Sebastian Blackwood|Blackwood]].

TJ seems like a goodhearted and slightly foolish guy. Blackwood seems like your regular silver spoon rich kid, but I'll keep my assumptions to myself, he might be different.

I went shooting with TJ, mighty fine shot he is. May be empty behind the eyes, but they themselves work wonders.

### Sat 5/7/1924
Since we're dealing with a larger beast here, I figured we'll need a ~~pit~~ reinforced pit-trap or perhaps a cage trap.

We spent the day driving to [[New Jersey]].

### Sun 6/7/1924
We started out by asking the local folk. Most knew nothing, but we found a season[?] slaughter. TJ identifies the tracks of the creature. A two-footed thing, about the heft of a large goat. No signs of claws or bite-marks, which indicates it stamps the creatures to death. → [[The Jersey Devil]]

We ended up building an ensnarement trap, using barbed wire and pulls to hopefully trap the beast.

No beast showed up during the night.
