## Transcript
### Friday 18-07-24 *(corrected from "Thursday")*
In the morning we went to the park, where we were assaulted by some strange men. They drove by, but no-one got hit, we killed the driver & shooter.

Luckily no bystanders got hurt.

[[Father Felix Oakley|The priest]] was there in the park, and after the kerfuffle he started making strange ramblings.

I stuffed his mouth with some gauze, which enraged him. After a slight brawl [[TJ]] shot and killed him. He exploded into wax. → [[Sanctified Wax]]

We rushed to [[Father Felix's Church|the church]].

When we arrived there was nothing. We took the wax off the fire, and brought it to the car, along with the Father's Bible. → [[Bible of Father Felix]], [[The Everliving Flame]]

After we drove to [[Houston]], where TJ has a contact. We arrive at [[Maxwell Gibson|Gibson's]] @ afternoon, but he wasn't home. Since he were not home, we went to the station, we met with the sheriff who told us Gibson was dead.

I spent some time reading the Bible of Father Felix. While most of it was in Latin, it became clear that he not only knew spells, but also crafted certain items. Ask Sebastian to interpret.

TJ & [[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]] had been looking through Maxwell's again, found a letter indicating that some "J" got a purchase[?] from Maxwell. → [[Mr. J]]

![[a22-bible-of-father-felix.jpg|220]]
*The Bible of Father Felix.*

Sebastian read the book and found that the Father had encircled the passages about light. He also wrote of needing a congregation to contain the angel that descended to earth. → [[The Angel]]

We ended the day by visiting some gun shop apparently TJ's a regular here. Same guy ~~that~~ robbed the store, TJ & Sebastian seemed to know him.
