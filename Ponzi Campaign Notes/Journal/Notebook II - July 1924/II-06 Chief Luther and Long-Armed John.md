# II-06 · Chief Luther and Long-Armed John (19–20 July 1924)

> [!summary] At a glance
> - [[Gates]]: re-animation is "not an unusual thing". **[[Chief Luther]] has been shot dead in the street.**
> - Morgue: [[Long-Armed John]]'s autopsy — fungal growth, slimy — and his body vanished. Gates had told Luther to bury the Longarm evidence. Police ([[Jaylin Baker]], [[Hernandez]]) report grave robbers; everything points to [[The Chapters (Mob)|the mobsters]].
> - [[Arthur's Belongings]] recovered. "Feeding" [[The Compass|TJ's compass]] locates John in **[[Boston]]**.
> - The team ambushes John in Boston, bags him and flees the Mafia all day.

## Transcript
### Saturday 19-07-24
When we awoke in the morning [[Gates]] was there. From him we found that re-animation was not an unusual thing.

![[a23-chief-luther-chalk-outline.jpg|220]]
*Chief Luther was killed on the street.* → [[Chief Luther]]

[[TJ]] & [[Sebastian Blackwood|Blackwood]] figured we'd go ask at the station to see what's what.

The chief, seemed a kind, but stressed man, who told us that it was a good shot.

We went to the morgue after, to look for clues about [[Long-Armed John|John Longarm]]. The doctor found the autopsy report for us, told us it was a strange case. He was covered in some fungal growth, slightly slimy. Strangely his body was removed without anyone knowing.

Gates ~~[struck]~~ had directed [[Chief Luther|Luther]] to bury the evidence for the Longarm case. Back at the station we met up with [[Jaylin Baker]] & [[Hernandez]] to ask about the body. There are a lot of reports of grave robbers, who dig people up and remove the body. → [[Ghouls (The Dogs)]]?

Apparently no-one received the body from the morgue. Everything is pointing to the mobsters. → [[The Chapters (Mob)]]

We needed more evidence, so we went to the crime scene to look for blood, or other bodily fluids, but no luck.

![[a23-john-walked-off.jpg|240]]
*Maybe John just walked off himself.*

TJ remembered that [[Arthur Wakefield|Arthur]] might've had some things. In his possessions we find: → [[Arthur's Belongings]]
- A heavy leather jacket, metal playing cards, empty vials, a vial of dark goop, and a vial of blood?

After feeding TJ's compass, we calculated John to live in Boston. → [[The Compass]]

### Sunday 20-07-24
We triangulated Long Armed John to be in [[Boston]]. After arriving Sebastian & I went to take down John. We went up the stairs, and ambushed him.

After he was shot, all hell went up[?]. Sebastian got a getaway car, and I shot one of the gangsters.

After we grabbed John, stuffed him in a sack, and ran out the door.

We ran to the ~~[struck]~~ car, to escape the Mafia..

Almost the whole day was spent running away.

![[a23-john-on-the-roof.jpg|240]]
*John on the roof... Dead.*
