## Transcript
### Tuesday 29-07-24 *(written "29-09-24")*
In the night [[William Charles Ponzi|Ponzi]] came to me to confirm that his wife & kid would survive. → [[Martha Ponzi]]

In the morning the wife & child were delivered on the back of two Nile crocodiles. Strange.

The sun started purging the two, enveloping them in a light stronger than any I've ever seen. → [[Purifying Flame]]

*(on a pasted slip:)*
As the day went on, one of the lights almost went out, but one of [[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]]'s staff had his back. But as he entered the circle he, and the candle, went into flame.

![[b05-purification-pentagram.jpg|360]]
*The ritual circle: a pentagram with candles at two of the points.*

We would need traps, lodging & anything else needed.

At the end of the ritual Ponzi told me the beast would be in the [[Blackwood Forest]].
