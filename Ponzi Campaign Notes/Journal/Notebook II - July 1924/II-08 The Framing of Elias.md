# II-08 · The Framing of Elias (24–26 July 1924)

> [!summary] At a glance
> - The team postpones purifying Ponzi's family until Ponzi talks to TJ & Sebastian; [[Jimmy]] is no help.
> - [[Tommy]] brings a newspaper: the head of **Chapter 3** has been caught — it's TJ's friend **[[Elias]]**. The [[Photographs of Elias|photographic evidence]] shows a newspaper dated **4 June** — later than the alleged date of **25 April**. Lawyer: [[Otis]].
> - [[Peterson]] → [[Fowler]] (sure Elias was *there* to be photographed) → a socialite party where [[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]] meets **[[Dallas]]** of the mob. A woman invited Sebastian to get to [[TJ]].
> - The narrator beats a doorman-brawler, [[Laurie]], grabs pictures from his pocket, and they fight their way out.
> - Dallas is held at the estate, bribes staff, then is locked in **[[Jimmy's Vault]]**. A car from [[Saltonstall]] was to pick him up.
> - Recurring dream: shadowy men chasing the narrator across the prairie — the curse of [[The Shirt|the armor]].

## Transcript
### Thursday 24/07/1924
At the breakfast table we decided not to purify [[William Charles Ponzi|Ponzi]]'s family until he spoke with [[TJ]] & [[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]] so we can figure out what exactly he wants. → [[Purifying Flame]]

We called on [[Jimmy]] to inform us on why Ponzi won't speak to TJ & Sebastian.

Jimmy did not provide much useful information.

TJ had a visitor this morning. [[Tommy]] had found a newspaper from a week ago, which mentioned that the head honcho of Chapter 3 had been caught. Issue is, it's one of TJ's friends. → [[Elias]], [[The Chapters (Mob)]]

@ the court we learned of photographic evidence of him with the Chapter. → [[Photographs of Elias]]

After we went to the jail to talk to Elias. Elias has no recollection of any of the evidence presented against him. And his lawyer did not exactly seem pleased with how the day had gone.

Something about the pictures seemed off, TJ noticed there was a paper on one of the pictures, and when we went to the library the paper was from the 4th of June, much later

*(the pasted Bible handout interrupts here — see [[II-07 The Bible and the Everliving Flame]])*

than the 25th of April. [[Otis]] did not seem convinced that it's damning tho.

The last thing we needed to do, was to figure out if the police are cashing[?] on this.

### Friday 25-07-24
We started the day by looking for an old colleague of TJ, Mr. [[Peterson]]. His employee mentioned he would probably be at [[The Tavern|the Tavern]], a local "watering hole".

When we got there it seems I was ID'd as a cop or something. I wasn't allowed in. Instead I went ~~in~~ to fetch TJ, so we could look for [[Fowler]] instead.

We went to his house and learned that Fowler was absolutely sure that Elias was there to be photographed.

![[b03-tj-bush-method.jpg|240]]
*TJ taught me a neat new hiding method.*

We met with Sebastian, who met with Peterson, he learned of a socialite party.

Sebastian was pulled into a meeting where he met with [[Dallas]] from the mob. Apparently he had a contact in one of the local banks.

In the party I spoke with an elder guy and a lady who knew of us. ~~Apparently~~ It seems that she invited Sebastian to get her hands on TJ, maybe due to the whole "mob-thing".

While waiting, one of the door men started moving towards me. This is definitely a "mob-thing".

The guy was called [[Laurie]][?]. Apparently he's a bit of a fighter, and wanted a fist fight. Apparently this old dog's gotta show his teeth.

After beating him I grabbed some pictures from his pocket. But then a bunch of guys ran into the room. After beating 5 of them & TJ I[?] we grabbed our things & ran.

After getting out, I couldn't find TJ, nor Sebastian, so I ran back into the hotel. However, he wasn't there, so we ran to the car, where Sebastian was waiting.

We decided to keep Dallas at the estate. → [[Blackwood Estate]]

During the night I dreamt of being chased by shadowy men across the prairie. I have to figure out how to rid this curse, so that I can use this armor without issue. → [[The Shirt]], [[The Prairie]]

### Saturday 26/07/24
Dallas met us at the breakfast table. We started interrogating him after we found out that he bribed a staff with some money to get them to deliver a message.

I thought we could have learned of why Elias came to be framed, but Dallas was unwilling to spill any beans. He did however tell us that a car from [[Saltonstall|Selton Stall]][?] would come pick him up.

In the end we decided to put Dallas in a cell in [[Jimmy's Vault]].

We went to [[Austin]] to ensure that we had an alibi. Sebastian went to the Austin Statesman to speak ill of the hotel's security.
