# II-07 · The Bible and the Everliving Flame (21–23 July 1924)

> [!summary] At a glance
> - Gun shop: TJ gets his gun back; the narrator and Sebastian buy automatic rifles. [[Only Betsy]] noted as TJ's favourite Austin haunt.
> - [[Bible of Father Felix|The Bible]] yields instructions for consuming an angel and three spells: [[Purifying Flame|Cleansing fire]] (what Ponzi wants), [[Ardens Rancor|Fireball]], [[Invocatio Solis Invicti|Magnify]]; plus [[Investment of Fire]].
> - [[The Everliving Flame]] is carried by hand to the drawing-room hearth at the [[Blackwood Estate]].
> - Casting costs sanity; [[Ghouls (The Dogs)|"the dogs"]] live in the Blackwood basement. A [[Sigil Ring]] imbues the narrator's [[Elephant Gun]] with fire.
> - **[[William Charles Ponzi|Ponzi]] visits the narrator at night**: cast the cleansing on his wife & child; in return, a hint to find the **Black Forest Beast**.
> - Pasted handout: [[Handout - Bible of Father Felix]].

## Transcript
### Monday 21-07-24
We started the day by going to a bespoke gunshop, so [[TJ]] could get his gun back. And we could deliver Tallulah[?]. [[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]] and I also got some automated rifles.

Later we studied [[Bible of Father Felix|the Bible of Father Felix]].

We found instructions on how to consume one of [[The Angel|the angels]], as well as ~~two~~ three spells.
1. Cleansing fire (what Ponzi wanted) → [[Purifying Flame]]
2. Fireball (send homing fire) → [[Ardens Rancor]]
3. Magnify (magnifies sunlight to burn things) → [[Invocatio Solis Invicti]]

Furthermore, we read of enchantments where one way was to enchant a container, and all things in that container gets imbued with fire. → [[Investment of Fire]]

![[a23-only-betsy.jpg|220]]
*"Only Betsy" — a strip club in Austin TJ was fond of.* → [[Only Betsy]]

*(notebook p.8)*
We wanted to move the fire from Texas to the estate, so that we could explore the spells of the "Bible".

We arrived at [[Father Felix's Church|the church]] in the evening.

The fire could only be moved by a human, and while it was hot, it wasn't so hot that we couldn't get it moved to the hearth in the drawing room. → [[The Everliving Flame]], [[Blackwood Estate]]

### Tuesday 22-07-24
Using the fire requires sacrifice, apparently SB ([[Sebastian Blackwood]]) knows an eccentric is into these kinds of things.

He gave us a small walk through of the costs of using these spells. The mental fortitude of the caster.

I went with Sebastian to the basement to talk to the dogs in the basement. I don't really understand the dilemma, why not just build an annex for them. → [[Ghouls (The Dogs)]]

They looked grotesque, would make a great trophy if I can catch just one of them.

We got a ring with the sigils, and used it to imbue fire enhancement to my elephant gun. → [[Sigil Ring]], [[Elephant Gun]], [[Investment of Fire]]
