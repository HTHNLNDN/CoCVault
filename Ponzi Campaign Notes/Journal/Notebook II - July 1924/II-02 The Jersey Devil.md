## Transcript
### Monday | 7/7/1924
When we got up [[TJ]] was still missing, apparently this is normal. I went to gather info. Nothing of use, just met a gal, hunter gal. I did however see smoke in the trees, perhaps someone's needing help.

Apparently it was TJ, he had found a great circle of effigies, which inside in their middle was an elder forest dweller, [[Helga]], who seems to be the one who put out the call for the hunt of [[The Jersey Devil|the Jersey Devil]]. She knew of the devil & its location.

After gathering supplies we found the shortcut Helga spoke of. There was a strange opening where hunters hung from the air. A strange sight, not something you see... ever.

I was almost captured, but felt my mind contract, almost repelling whatever came after me. As the fixation of my mind left me, I came to see that the strings hung all over from the sky. I found a safe path.

We came upon a cave, where the beast resided. It was a devil goat with wings of sorts, we captured it, and moved it to [[Shelly's Facility|Shelly's]] through the teleporter gates prepared for us.

[[Shelly]] told us to move the beast to section 'C' – the humanoid section. He told us to follow the Medusa-protocol. I couldn't keep my eyes to myself, and through one of the windows I saw a colony of small fungus resembling[?] humans. Rather strange, not sure what to do with this. → [[Fungal Humans]]

Shelly told us about [[The Bureau|the Bureau]], which has two [*sic* — three are listed] departments. **Prometheus**, the researchers. **Daedalus**, the containment unit. **Astraea**, internal police.

It seems I'm stuck here for the time being. As any deserters seem to [be] picked up and left for dead[?] here at 312 Main Street, Coarn[?], NY. → [[Shelly's Facility]]
