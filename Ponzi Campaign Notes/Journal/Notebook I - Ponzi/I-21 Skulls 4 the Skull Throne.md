# I-21 · Skulls 4 the Skull Throne

> [!summary] At a glance
> - Ponzi gets the gems and completes [[The Jewelled Skull|the skull]] — then runs.
> - He wakes in the [[Catacombs under Corpus Christi]]. A broad man knows about **"Eduardo"** (Ponzi's old vacation-home scam down South).
> - He meets **[[Hopkins]]** — 2 m tall, dog-faced, in a stretched suit ([[Ghouls (The Dogs)|a ghoul]]) — who wants help getting a house in Argentina.
> - As the skull predicted, [[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]] tries to take the skulls.

## Transcript
I got the gems, and finished [[The Jewelled Skull|the skull]], it's a trap[?]. I can't believe the power I've acquired: and then I ran.

![[a15-dungeons-under-the-city.jpg|240]]
*Dungeons under the City.* → [[Catacombs under Corpus Christi]]

When I came to a large man was speaking to me. He claimed that he knew of Eduardo, shit. I never thought that would catch up to me here, I only ~~ran~~ ran the vacation home scam further down South. But how it's come back to haunt me.

The broad guy brought me to [[Hopkins]], a man, or... dog? The creature was 2 meters tall, broad as a door, with a suit stretched thin as sheets. His face? Was elongated, much like that of a herd dog, but without the ~~hair~~ fur. → [[Ghouls (The Dogs)]]

He did not want ~~me to~~ hurt me, the dense guy just wanted me to help him get his house. How hard could that be? It's a house in Argentina, sure, a little expensive, but I'm sure the very fair Argentinian government can help a fella who can rustle their memories.

After "friend" led me out of the catacombs what the skull said came true. It knew they wanted the skulls, and [[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]] tried to take them. He used some lame excuse, but he clearly just wanted the skulls. The rich bastard does not know when to stay out of things, but I guess that's to be expected from a gold spooned child.
