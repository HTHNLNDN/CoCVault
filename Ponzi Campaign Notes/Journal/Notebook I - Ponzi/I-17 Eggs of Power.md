## Transcript
In the immediate aftermath, our three foo... protagonists decided to investigate the news regarding sightings of what they have named the 'Dragon'. → [[The Dragon (Byakhee)]]

A local news story pointed them towards a sighting 20 years earlier.

The story spoke of the same creature, but the interviewed mentioned having seen a man riding the creature. → [[The Marsh Wizard of Ipswich]]

———

> In the night I saw, a meteor.
> Behind the great light, a fright.
> At once I rised, ~~the sk~~ the sky, my sky, the earth, my earth.
> In the dirt I saw, the creatures who swarm.
> A creature of more, and I fell, the visions I bore.
> From a book I read, how the world has turned red.
> From the book I learned, the adversary I yearned.
> At once I was borne, amidst the pit of scorn.
> And in this pit I found, my longing, the creatures, the ones who seek truth of sound.
