## Transcript
Outside the shop I met one of the high priests, [[Jeremiah]], a kind man it seems, he gave me a letter to hand to [[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]].

After some time one of the [[Fish People|fish people]] fell through the roof of the car. He was trying to pilfer something, anything, but he didn't get anything.

> "A letter to G-man, hey. I've fled. At this point we need to clean up. Let's get working on this debt. We clear that and we are free to do whatever we need to entertain ourselves." → [[Jeremiah's Letter]], [[G-Man]]
