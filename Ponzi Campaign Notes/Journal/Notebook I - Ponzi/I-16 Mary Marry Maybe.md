> **Superstitions and Folk Lore of Essex County ~ 76**
> The infamous Marsh Wizard of Ipswich has been haunting Essex County for centuries according to many local accounts. Evidence of his existence goes back to at least the 18th century where accounts of disappearing cattle and people were associated with the Marsh Wizard. Such accounts include the notion that the wizard had a demon familiar, as the flapping of giant wings was reported on more than one occasion.
> The tales grow more outlandish over time, with some claiming that the Marsh Wizard had a dragon-like steed that he uses to ride the night skies and terrorize local residents.
> The illustration below is an artist's interpretation of testimony given by a young man named Andrew Leman, who claims to have seen the wizard riding the beast over the salt marshes one night in 1906.
