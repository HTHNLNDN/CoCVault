The only way to get out of this situation was to run back to my friends.

I've hidden [[The Jewelled Skull|the skulls]], but [[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]] can't know that. Maybe if I give him some fake skulls, he'll help me with the damn dogs.

I made two replicas and smashed them.

When I got up from down below, [[TJ]] & [[Jimmy]] were there. How perfect! I'm pretty sure I convinced Jimmy that I destroyed the real skulls. He told us to come along, and at this point I might as well follow, I need medical attention.

Sebastian picked us up from the Dover experience and took us to the main [[Blackwood Estate]]. I told them about the dogs, but they did not understand. → [[Ghouls (The Dogs)]]

When we woke the day after I tried again to convince them, however they clearly did not believe me.

Or maybe they just do not weigh the situation as I do? Honestly, they are kind folk, and I ought to give them the benefit of doubt, I'm sure they just don't underst[and]...
