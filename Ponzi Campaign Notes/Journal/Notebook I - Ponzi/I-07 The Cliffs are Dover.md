## Transcript
I woke up in a cell, [[TJ]], [[Delila Arlington|Delila]], and even [[Arthur Wakefield|Arthur]] was there, seems ~~this~~ [[Keeser]][?] is out to get us. To be fair, I don't think we got much of a case. Clearly we've committed a crime. But [[B. Fisher]] seems to be here to save our asses. He's handing us over to a certain [[Jimmy]].

![[a05-scroll-tenets.jpg|260]]
*Scroll: "I. ABOVE ALL ELSE STANDS THE ONE — II. WE ARE BEYOND TIME & SPACE"*

### Letter → [[Ponzi's Letter to Martha]]
> Dearest [[Martha Ponzi|Martha]],
> I'm glad to hear y'all are doing well, I have good news.
> While every day away from you both feels like an eternity, I have found hope, I'm certain this will solve everything.
> And I swear to God almighty when I tell you this is no scheme, no lie, nothing of the sort. There is this tonic, some newfangled science, that lets me peer into the future.
> I know not yet what exactly we can use this for, but trust your husband, when I tell you that this will be our fortune.
> Yours forever, with love
> W. C. Ponzi

*(Faint pencil drawing at the foot of the page: a lighthouse by the sea under a sky of eyes.)*
