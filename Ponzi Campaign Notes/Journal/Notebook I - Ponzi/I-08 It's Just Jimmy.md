# I-08 · It's Just Jimmy

> [!summary] At a glance
> - [[Jimmy]] is from "[[The Bureau]]", department **Prometheus**, which keeps records on strange beings.
> - Ponzi steals [[The Black]] from [[TJ]]; he and [[Arthur Wakefield|Arthur]] want to use its power. *(Addiction is setting in.)*
> - [[The Old Man in the Asylum]] talks of the "Sleepers" and gives a riddle: "Go west to find the East." → [[The Sleepers and the Dreamer]]
> - [[Dr. Chase]] is seen alive at [[The University (Arkham)|the university]].

## Transcript
[[Jimmy]] seems to come from something called "[[The Bureau|the Bureau]]". A strange organisation that deals with the occult. He comes from some Department called Prometheus. They seem to be the ones who collect records on these strange beings.

![[a06-grand-wizard-jimmy.jpg|220]]
*Grand Wizard Jimmy releasing us.*

I swiped [[The Black|the Black]] from [[TJ]], and I feel awful about it, but I have to have it, he doesn't understand it. It's too precious for him. After church [[Arthur Wakefield|Arthur]] met me at [[The Safehouse|the safehouse]]. Arthur gets it, he understands the power, he understands that we need to utilise this for our own good. It's okay, I can trust him, at least until I get out of this fog, he wants the same, I'm sure of it.

We found an old man in [[The Asylum|the asylum]], he spoke of these "Sleepers", he had drawings, drawings of people, sigils. Arthur said he could feel the drawings. Clearly the old man knows something. He waits for the "Sleepers" to come for him, but what are they? → [[The Old Man in the Asylum]], [[The Sleepers and the Dreamer]], [[The Sigils]]

> Soon it's time, tomorrow, yesterday, all are needed. They will come for you, everyone, look to the water east, to the west, west to the east, go east. Go west to find the East. They come because they need.

I went to [[The University (Arkham)|the university]] to look for Black, I know, shameful, I luckily didn't find any, but guess who I saw. [[Dr. Chase]]. I wonder how [[Walter]] is doing?
