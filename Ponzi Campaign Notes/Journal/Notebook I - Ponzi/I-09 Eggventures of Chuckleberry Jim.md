> [!summary] At a glance

## Transcript
We've been given our first task. It seems we've been sent on a quest to fetch some stolen items, a Greek text, the translation of it and some Innsmouth gold coins. → [[The Stolen Greek Text]], [[Innsmouth Gold Coins]]

While investigating [[The Museum|the museum]] we discovered that the coins were targeted[?]. We did not find much else. After this we went to talk to [[Dr. Stanley]]. He did a translation of a Greek book on math. The book spoke of mapping[?] geometries, the sun & the moon, symbols etc.

![[a06-greek-symbol-wheel.jpg|240]]
*A wheel divided into segments with symbols (Θ, Π, B, A, Λ, 3, E, J…) and an arrow — likely from the Greek text.*

After a bit of discussion, [[Arthur Wakefield|Arthur]], [[TJ]] and I split up, Arthur had a method of contacting [[Jimmy]]. TJ and I went to see if anyone was home at [[Armitage|Armitage's]]. Conclusion: none, so we figured we had to contact Jimmy.

When we got home, there was Arthur... **Dead**. How did this happen? [[The Black]]? I knew this was possible, but hell, I can't believe he's gone...

We're at a loss, but we must move on.

We got in contact with [[Dr. Chase]], he'd come back. He spoke of seeing how people died of Black, he didn't want to disclose what exactly he saw, seems traumatized. [[Walter]] and Chase's research was stolen from him.

We searched [[Jacob Dover|Dover's]], we thought he might be the perpetrator, but alas, another dead end.

The last option seems to figure who robbed these places. After going to [[The Sycamore]] we got to speaking with [[Lexy Romano]], who showed us [[Spoon]].
