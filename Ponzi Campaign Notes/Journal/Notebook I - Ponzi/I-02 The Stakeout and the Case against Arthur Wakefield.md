# I-02 · The Stakeout & The Case against Arthur Wakefield

> [!summary] At a glance
> - Stakeout at [[The Slaughterhouse]]: expensive car, a lookout, and a creature crawling in the rafters.
> - [[Arthur Wakefield]] turns up with a stranger; [[Chief Luther|Luther]] has him arrested for aggravated assault.
> - [[Long-Armed John|John Longarms]] visits Arthur's cell looking for "Thomson" ([[TJ]]?).
> - Ponzi argues the case; Arthur gets off with public disturbance and a **$50 fine due 31 May**.

## Transcript
At the stakeout we saw a rather expensive car outside [[The Slaughterhouse|the slaughterhouse]]. Someone inside is keeping watch, and a strange creature was crawling under the rafters.

It seems [[Arthur Wakefield|Arthur]] had been sneaking around our backs; as he showed up with some unknown person out of nowhere, that would explain how [[Jacob Dover|Jacob]] came to know my name, or perhaps he just had the same idea as [[TJ]] and I. Either way then it's fishy to bring some new guy into this without us knowing.

We brought Arthur into the [[Arkham Police Station|station]], on the request of [[Chief Luther|Luther]]. Arthur was arrested on the charges of aggravated assault.

### The case against Arthur Wakefield
Lied about reason for getting in court[?], [[Paul Rodgers|Rodgers]] not to take stand as he was missing.

I spent a day off looking at similar cases, it seems highly unlikely that Arthur will be persecuted for aggravated assault (*McAlister v. Ashland*[?]).

Arthur was visited in the night (Tue.) by [[Long-Armed John|John Longarms]], he was looking for Thomson ([[TJ]]?). TJ visited, apparently there had been a riot the other night also. After, T.J. went to a hotel without telling me... B12[?] am I right?! ... Paid using ill-gotten gains.

The closing statement seemed to go well. Judge was not unimpressed at least. Clearly he was no fan of neither Wakefield nor Rodgers. Wakefield was dismissed on public disturbance, a fine of $50 to be paid by the 31st of May.
