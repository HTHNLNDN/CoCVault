# I-01 · Mystery of the Ponzi Potion

> [!summary] At a glance
> - Ponzi is accused of selling [[The Black]], a deadly drug that has killed [[Rodrick]] and two students, and apparently [[Dr. Chase]].
> - A deal with [[Dallas]] goes wrong when [[TJ]] and the police burst in; car chase, Dallas escapes.
> - We meet [[Delila Arlington|Delila "Darling" Arlington]], who traces the Black to [[The University (Arkham)|the university]] and its seller [[Paul Rodgers]].
> - Someone is impersonating Ponzi from [[The Slaughterhouse]] (143 River Street), owned by [[Jacob Dover]]. [[Dover's Book]] reveals his vendetta.

### Mystery of the Ponsi Potion (cont.)
Darling took initiative, prodding around the night-life, she found that "Black" was centered around [[The University (Arkham)|the university]].

Everyone who had "Black" tongues seem to group together at the party. Arthur found many empty vials. The Black seller has a route. He goes on Thursdays & Fridays around the medical campus. **[[Paul Rodgers]]** is his name.

![[a02-the-boys-at-the-party.jpg|440]]
*The Boys at the party (labelled: TJ, William, Delila…).*

Paul Rodgers seemed a scared fella, but of who? He did not seem to know the [[Dallas|Texas gangsters]], so it must be his supplier he's scared of. He was impossible to work with, so we found his address: Barnton[?] Street.

[[The Slaughterhouse]] seems to be where whoever impersonates me is.

The slaughterhouse is located on River Street 143, it was abandoned a while back but bought by Mr. Dover, [[Jacob Dover|Jacob]]. We broke into his home, and under the bed we found a clothbound book → [[Dover's Book]].

The book spoke of Jacob's mom, who had a lung disease it seems. In her last moments of life her husband was desperate, and I sold her a drug. Of course it didn't work, she died, and now he has a vendetta against me.
