# I-06 · The Cliffs of Dover

> [!summary] At a glance
> - Ponzi comes to outside a barn with his fist on [[Delila Arlington|Delila]] and [[Arthur Wakefield|Arthur]] nearly dead. [[Dover's Book]] is burned; the party flees.
> - [[TJ]] has been **cursed**. Delila "knows of lots of secret peoples".
> - The final assault on [[The Slaughterhouse]] with Delila and [[Dallas]] backing them up. The building burns.
> - [[Jacob Dover|Dover]] performs a moonless summoning ([[Dover's Ritual]]); **TJ kills Dover**.

## Transcript
What the fuck just happened? All of a sudden I'm standing[?] outside a barn, [[Delila Arlington|Delila]] at the end of my fist, [[Arthur Wakefield|Arthur]] more or less dead.

Christ, [[Jacob Dover|Dover]] is here, we're fucked, I need to get everyone out of here. Arthur's down, [[Dover's Book|the book]] is burned, we fled. But what now?

Arthur's been fixed up, but still struggling, and apparently Delila knows of lots of secret peoples!

Fuck! [[TJ]]'s been cursed, I think leaving the book was a mistake. Dover is clearly gonna go for us. But how the hell are we gonna do anything? He has demonic powers!

You know? It's 'bout time we decided to pony up and just beat the crap out of him.

Just as TJ and I got a hell of a plan, even got Delila, [[Dallas]]... it's[?] backing us up. However, Dover saw us coming, he was going on some ramble, honestly I didn't listen, I was panicking.

But to make a long story short, the ~~Barn's~~ [[The Slaughterhouse|slaughterhouse]] is on fire, TJ & Delila's a-shootin', and folks are dying, guess Dover wasn't as dangerous as he seemed.

When I woke, Dover was over by TJ, I tried to shoot, but as sharp as I am, I ain't no sharpshooter.
