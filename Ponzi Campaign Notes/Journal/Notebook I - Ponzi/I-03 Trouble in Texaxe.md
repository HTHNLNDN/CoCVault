# I-03 · Trouble in Texaxe

> [!summary] At a glance
> - [[TJ]] sends a note: gangsters are after him at a hotel on Main Street. On the way, *a man falls from the sky*.
> - The mob is after TJ. [[Chief Luther|Luther]] hides that he knows [[B. Fisher]].
> - Ponzi researches [[Jacob Dover|Dover]]'s father's ramblings at [[The University (Arkham)|the university library]] — none of the stories are local.
> - [[Arthur Wakefield|Arthur]] found bloody and "out of it" by the river; [[Delila Arlington|Delila]] keeps lookout.

## Transcript
![[a03-tj-gangster-note.jpg|260]]
*TJ sent a note, apparently he's been caught... The note reads: "GANGSTERS AFTER ME AT HOTEL ON MAIN STREET, NAME …, ROOM …"*

[[TJ]] seems to have got himself into a heap of trouble, we went to find him, but on the way a man fell from the sky.

We was told TJ were at the [[Arkham Police Station|station]]. TJ is terrified, [[The Chapters (Mob)|the mob]] is after him and he doesn't know what to do.

We decided to look into [[Jacob Dover|Dover]], and his father's strange ramblings. I got into [[The University (Arkham)|the university library]], but found nothing on the first day. TJ spoke to [[Chief Luther|Luther]], who was trying to hide his knowing of [[B. Fisher]]. Why? Who knows?... On further examination I've found that none of these stories are local.

As I left the ~~window~~ library a bush was staring at me, rather strange. That bush was apparently TJ in disguise, not a very good one that is.

![[a03-secret-tj-bush.jpg|220]]
*Secret TJ Bush.*

He told me [[Arthur Wakefield|Arthur]] was kidnapped, I call bull, he's just a lil eccentric.

We meet up with [[Delila Arlington|Delila]] to find Arthur, patched up the car, and went to look for him.

Delila kept a lookout, whilst we found him, walking along the bank of the river. He was bloody, obviously, so we took him to the hospital, he seemed fine after.

TJ seems awfully bothered by Arthur, and honestly I get it, he seems out of it, like really out of it.

![[a03-delila-never-speak-of-this.jpg|260]]
*Delila Arlington — "Listen Kiddo, I owe 1 and you owe one now. NEVER SPEAK OF THIS."*
