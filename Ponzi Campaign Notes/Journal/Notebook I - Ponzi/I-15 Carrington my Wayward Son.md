# I-15 · Carrington my Wayward Son

> [!summary] At a glance
> - [[Eves]] briefs the team: [[The Carringtons]] suffer vandalism the police ignore.
> - Their fountain (boy on two dolphins) was smashed by something with claws; in its place, pearlescent banana-shaped "stones" → [[Dragon Eggs]]. Breaking one releases a rotten-egg gas.
> - Only missing staff member: [[Albert Hackett]] (also written "Alfred"), whose hotel paper is suspiciously fine.

← [[I-14 Corpus Christi TX]] · Next → [[I-16 Mary Marry Maybe]]

## Transcript
We met with [[Eves]], who briefed us on the situation. Apparently [[The Carringtons]] have been experiencing vandalism, and the police don't do nothing.

The Carringtons had a fountain broken, the fountain resembled a small boy standing on two dolphins.

![[a11-smashed-fountain.jpg|220]]
*The Smashed Fountain.* → [[Carrington Mansion]]

The day after the statue was broken, and in its place lie these 'special' stones, almost like bananas but in these brilliant pearlescent colors. → [[Dragon Eggs]]

I tried to study it over, but accidentally broke a piece of it.. When it broke a liquid evaporated, and the gas it produced stank of rotten eggs.

The whole crew seemed decent, the only person who's been missing is [[Albert Hackett]], who's sick for the day.

The fountain seemed to have been massacred by some monster, there were claw marks and the statue seemed to have been thrown.

With Alfred being our only lead, we decided to pay him a visit.

While at his hotel room I noticed that the paper he was using was of a superior quality, much nicer than someone of his caliber should have. Something ain't right about this guy.
