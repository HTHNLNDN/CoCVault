# I-11 · Apartmentdox

> [!summary] At a glance
> - [[Jimmy]] briefly pulls the team away ("lairs of snakes"; a team of 3 is too small).
> - [[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]] joins — bookish, rich, "a whole estate just for this job".
> - Inside [[22 Curzon Street]]: a looping, superliminal space → [[The Apartment Paradox]]. A dead family, a monster, a screaming woman (shot), a [[The Spinning Coin|spinning coin]].
> - Sebastian's chant ends the paradox. **[[Sticky Jack]] is dead**; recovered the [[Innsmouth Gold Coins|coins]] and the [[The Stolen Greek Text|translation]] — the Greek text itself still missing.

← [[I-10 A Slim Chance of Shady]] · Next → [[I-12 The Liar, the Snitch and the Wardrobe]]

## Transcript
We arrived at the complex of [[Sticky Jack]], finally a task that can just be one and done.

![[a07-endless-building.jpg|260]]
*This building goes on forever.*

Before we could go [[Jimmy]] escorted us away, spoke of lairs of snakes, seems a team of 3 is too little for our kind of tasks.

The new team member's a rich son-of-a-gun, already more useful than Arthur. Seems this fella spent his time on the books rather than playing with cards, my kinda guy. He had a whole estate just for this job! Reminds me of the time I sold fairy-drink to a, what was his name? GATSBY! → [[Sebastian Blackwood]]

Anywho, with a new competent team member under our belt → —||—

I fucking knew it was too good to be true... After hearing shots I decided to barge in, and once I went in, oh boy.

This whole apartment feels like reading Schopenhauer, ~~I read~~ I go to one part, and all of a sudden I'm back to where I started, still not understanding anything!

This whole place seems to exist in some superliminal space, everything is black, all that is here repeats itself, nothing is real. → [[The Apartment Paradox]]

A dead family, a monster, counting walls, none of it is real, yet it's our reality...

Wait, is this a test? Is the book even here?

### (cont.)
Yep, this is most definitely not real, there's something behind a door, screeching, twisting? Why is it in here? What is it, you know what, it doesn't matter, it's not real.

We found a live person, but the second they spotted us, she leapt and screamed. I shot her, Sebastian too, it got quiet, did we kill her?

![[a08-tentacles-door.jpg|240]]
*Tentacles bursting through a doorway.*

I saw it, I, but it didn't see me. I'm sure, it's real, but not existent? Ok, I'm not gonna look again.

[[Delila Arlington|Delila]] found a room, with a spinning coin, I caught it, but it might've broken my rib. → [[The Spinning Coin]]

[[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]] knew some chant that seemed to stop this paradoxical mess.

Once out we found ourselves in Sticky Jack's apartment. Jack was dead in the room besides us, with the transcript for the Greek text. So we got the coins, the translation and now we just need the text itself, which was apparently also with Jack.

So I guess we got our things, time to go, no need to dig any deeper.

As we got to the new home ([[255 West Derby Street]]) we were met by a whole crowd of people. A certain maître d'[?] seemed to look us up and down, I made sure to ask for their most expensive wine, always wanted to try a vintage one[?] and there's no way I'll get that opportunity again.
