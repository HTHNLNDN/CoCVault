# I-04 · TJs and Js: Suspects of Devilwork

> [!summary] At a glance
> - [[Arthur Wakefield|Arthur]] retrieves [[Dover's Book]] from the hospital and lies about it; [[TJ]] is furious and kicks him out. [[Delila Arlington|Delila]] runs off.
> - [[Jacob Dover|Dover]] ambushes Arthur: bring TJ to [[The Slaughterhouse]]. [[Long-Armed John]] tells TJ to meet at [[Independence Square]].
> - Arthur admits contact with the occult — "It's all devilwork."
> - Confrontation in the park; Long-Armed John dodges and flees.

## Transcript
He woke up in a sweat, distraught. I have no clue what to do with him. [[TJ]] neither, I hope he can come back. He went to the hospital to find [[Dover's Book|the book]], damn bastard. Honestly, I don't feel like I can trust him.

He came back, having clearly found the book, dang bastard lied to me. I hate this.

TJ seems to have a temper, he was furious for bringing the book into the house, I don't get it, and I won't try to. He kicked out [[Arthur Wakefield|Arthur]], everything seems to go to hell. Shit. Fuck. [[Delila Arlington|Delila]] seems to have run off, crazy girl.

---

Arthur tried to deposit the book, and when he went outside [[Jacob Dover|Dover]] was waiting for him. He is to bring TJ to [[The Slaughterhouse|the slaughterhouse]]. When on our way out of [[The Safehouse|the safehouse]] [[Long-Armed John]] was waiting by the door. Told TJ to meet him at [[Independence Square]][?].

I met up with Arthur, he told me that I'd been right all along. It's all devilwork. It seems he has had some contact with the occult, it's clearly messed a bit with him, must've been tough. It seems we are gonna go haywire on Dover tonight.

We went to the park, to see if we could help TJ. I found the guy, but after a missed shot, he ran for it. Arthur went up for the guy, but he dodged. Seems we are in the thick of it.
