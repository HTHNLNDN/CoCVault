# I-18 · Muddy Thoughts

> [!summary] At a glance
> - Ponzi's mind is slipping: "I'm the only one willing to sacrifice myself" — [[TJ]] and [[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]] pull guns on him.
> - The dragon is "banished". Ponzi completes "the first step": **he has a skull** → [[The Jewelled Skull]].
> - Believes [[Jimmy]] supports his experimenting; plans to bring a new skull to [[Mills Island]] and use Sebastian's greed.
> - Home visit: [[Martha Ponzi|Martha]] says he has **tattoos on his head** — "I knew I was chosen."

## Transcript
There was... a guy? But he's missing something, yes. He's not... complete... I have to, yes, I must. But what if... no, it doesn't matter, it must be done.

I think [[TJ]] and [[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]] are against me, they keep holding me back. As if they would ever understand the situation I'm in, they don't get it. I'm the only one willing to sacrifice myself, and what do I get? Two guns pointed right at me, I can't, I can't deal with them anymore, I can't wait for them. TJ is too slow, and Sebastian is simply too young.

We "banished" [[The Dragon (Byakhee)|the dragon]]? Not that it matters, is the problem solved? Was there a problem?

![[a14-five-skulls.jpg|260]]
*(A row of small skulls is drawn faintly here and on the next page.)*

`<--! I did it, the first step, I have one... --!>`

We've done it, it seems [[Jimmy]] is for us, me experimenting. He gets it, I have to, we all do, no choice. Now I have to get a new skull, and bring it to [[Mills Island]][?]. Perhaps I misjudged Sebastian, he's young, but greedy. And I can use that greed, perhaps if I can convince him that the dragon is a tool, but only I can control it. → [[The Jewelled Skull]]

I've got a small break, a little time at home, I miss it here, I wish I had more time at home.

[[Martha Ponzi|Martha]] mentioned me having tattoos on my head, this is it, I knew I was chosen. → [[The Sigils]]
