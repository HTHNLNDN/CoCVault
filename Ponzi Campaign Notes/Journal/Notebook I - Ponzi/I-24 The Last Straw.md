# I-24 · The Last Straw

> [!summary] At a glance
> - [[TJ]] is queasy after visiting the factory; Bently[?] sends him to the doctor on the outskirts.
> - Ponzi is suddenly in an office with **[[G-Man]]** — an Egyptian-looking man "with many names" — who advises him to get out of Innsmouth. Ponzi resolves to return to Alabama.
> - Underground caverns: [[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]] finds the [[Innsmouth Gold Forge|gold forge]], powered by the [[Prismatic Crystals|crystals first seen in Corpus Christi]].

← [[I-23 In Insmuth]] · Next → [[I-25 Dreamers Abound]]

## Transcript
[[TJ]] seemed rather queasy after visiting the factory. Bently[?] noticed, told me to take him to the doctor on the outskirts of town. Apparently he saw some old colleagues of his, I guess he has quite the past as well.

As he was falling I ran for him, and all of a sudden I found myself in an office. There sat a man, clearly of Egyptian descent. He said he had many names, I think I've found myself God, hence I called him G-man, just in case, but also leaving me with an alibi. He told me that he was there to give me advice, but it more or less came down to getting out of [[Innsmouth]]. I will honor this, and perhaps get back to Alabama, figure out what is happening. → [[G-Man]], [[Ponzi's House (Alabama)]]

![[a17-g-man-office.jpg|240]]
*G-Man sat in a beautiful office.*

But first we took to the underground. We found a large system of caverns. [[Sebastian Blackwood|Sebastian]] went up a hatch, and found the forge they use for gold. It appears to run off of the crystals we first saw in [[Corpus Christi]]. We should take it with us. → [[Innsmouth Gold Forge]], [[Prismatic Crystals]]

Before leaving we went to the shop, to tell [[The Innsmouth Shop Lady|the shop-lady]] what we knew.
