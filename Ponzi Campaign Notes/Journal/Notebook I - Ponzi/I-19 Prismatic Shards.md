## Transcript
On [[Mills Island|the island]] we found tracks showing that the Byakhee had been here earlier, seems with whatever's haunting the outskirts of the mansion ([[Carrington Mansion]]). → [[The Dragon (Byakhee)]]

[[TJ]] saw something in the water, and I decided to jump in. Old as I am getting, I twisted my knee, and sure enough a monster came for me.

The monster seemed to care much more for TJ than any of us other two. He was also the only one with a gun able to hurt it.

![[a14-blob-ascended.jpg|240]]
*A BIG BLOB ASCENDED towards us.* → [[The Lake Blob]]

After dealing with the monster I decided to have an extra look into the lake. Didn't seem like anything special.

*(A row of five small skulls.)*

When we got back I borrowed [[The Jewelled Skull|the skull]] and [[The Flute|flute]], and went to [[Corpus Christi]]. It's obvious that Mills[?] Island was never the right destination, of course the altar would be in Corpus Christi.

Clearly the reach of the island, however, it seems none have walked into the island.

I once again found Oscar[?], God he is beautiful. All clean and pristine. And the altar, I found it, the rest of it. And the skull, it's so ready, I just need... jewels, that's what it told me.

The night was interrupted by the guard dogs, TJ went to get us, and arriving at the scene I found a dog who had been split apart. Clearly a lucky strike by whatever five clawed beast struck it.
