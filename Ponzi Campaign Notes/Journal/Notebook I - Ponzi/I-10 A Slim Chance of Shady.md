# I-10 · A Slim Chance of Shady

> [!summary] At a glance
> - [[Spoon]] points to [[Sticky Jack]] and his friend [[Vince (The Spider)|Vince, "the Spider"]], who hangs around the university.

## Transcript
[[Spoon]]'s a greasy kind of fellow, but a dependable one. He pointed us towards a certain [[Sticky Jack]], and his friend Vince. Vince seemed to hang around [[The University (Arkham)|the university]]. Described as: A middle aged guy, clean shaven, black hair, suited up, much like me I suppose. The Spider is also an alias. → [[Vince (The Spider)]]

When we got back [[Jimmy]] was there, wanted to know about [[Arthur Wakefield|Arthur]], how he died, I don't get it, where's his humanity? He didn't seem to care, at least not beyond what was necessary for business.

I have to keep my humanity.

**[[22 Curzon Street]]**, that's where Jack's a-hiding. Seems Spider's a bit of a talker, all he needed was some [[The Black|Black]], 3 vials, I'm happy to be rid of it. Spider stole my damn wallet, once a thief, always one, even if trying to deal with him I guess.

~~I can't~~

Arthur, why did you have to die? I realise that can happen in this job, but still, why? Honestly you've made me reconsider this whole thing, I thought we had it figured out, I guess not. To hell with you Arthur, you just had to throw your life away, damn fool, wouldn't happen to the rest of us, he was too... curious?

We're moving! [[255 West Derby Street|West 255 Derby Street]]. Also [[Delila Arlington|Delila]] is back. Mansion living baby! Jimmy wants us to be 4 again. Already found us a replacement for Arthur, sick bastard. He introduced us to a **[[Sebastian Blackwood]]**, rich young guy it seemed. Not self made of course.
