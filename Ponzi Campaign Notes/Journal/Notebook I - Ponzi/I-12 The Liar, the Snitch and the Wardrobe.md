# I-12 · The Liar, the Snitch and the Wardrobe

> [!summary] At a glance
> - The party is formally in. [[Jimmy]] won't name the organisation ("there is power in a name"). Codenames in the field — e.g. **John Smith**; "Fisher" is one too ([[B. Fisher]]).
> - Final exam through [[The Magic Wardrobe]] into an old military base: a magically "boring" door ([[TJ]] temporarily blinded), a ghost calmed by speaking her name, an invisible minotaur maze → **[[The Compass]]**.
> - Ponzi reads [[Arthur's Notes]] and relives a vision of Halley's comet… and falls. → [[The Sigils]]

← [[I-11 Apartmentdox]] · Next → [[I-13 The Sigils]]

## Transcript
We finally made it in. Into what I don't exactly know, [[Jimmy]] won't give us a name, keeps saying that there is power in a name. I think he's being obtuse! If there's power in a name & this organisation doesn't have one, doesn't that make it powerless? → [[The Bureau]]

Apparently we are to go by codenames when operating. John Smith... how generic, but I guess that's the point.

Jimmy introduced us to our exam, apparently it's a final task before we're officially in.

He has a magic wardrobe, and once we entered we were in something that looked like an old military base, might be left from the Great War. → [[The Magic Wardrobe]]

First we had to open a door, a magically boring door that is. Genius that I am, I had it figured out instantly, of course I didn't want to steal everyone's thunder, so I let the other two take a shot at it, which was a clear mistake as it led to, what Jimmy called, a temporary blindness for [[TJ]].

Next task is retrieving some object from a haunted room, but what exactly was not very clear... guess we'll find out.

On a side note, if I ever get to have another code name like "Fisher" ([[B. Fisher]]), I'll choose something classy, like Clive Lewis perhaps.

Hell, if I ever get any free time I might have to write a book about our "adventures", it would probably need a lot of adaption to be palatable to the masses...

### (cont.)
Seems the ghost isn't unfriendly by definition. Speaking 'er name got her all in a bind, now she's just standing there. Still... I don't trust it...

Apparently that was all it took to pass the test, Jimmy asked some questions after, but honestly I didn't listen, just wanna get this over with and get paid.

Last test is about navigating some maze, seems Jimmy takes us for fools, I mean, I don't know 'bout the other two, but growing up in Alabama, I was surrounded by maize mazes.

...

Okay it's a little different, there's apparently a minotaurus in this maze? Invisible at that... luckily I'm sure minotaurs are bad as all hell.

Honestly this is no big deal, we just followed the maze around, and there it is! **The compass!** Easy as pie... → [[The Compass]]

—

All we needed to do was just wait a bunch, and then, we got out... no problems.

TJ handed me [[Arthur's Notes]]. After reading them I think I got it...

I was back... back to before things went wrong but it was different, there was an eerie silence. Me and [blank space] were looking up at Halley's and it was... so close... I was... so close... In its wake, its tail, I was... and I was not alone, something was there and I was... falling.
