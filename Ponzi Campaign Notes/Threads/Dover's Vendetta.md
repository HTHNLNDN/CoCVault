# Dover's Vendetta

> [!question] The question
> Who is impersonating Ponzi from the slaughterhouse, and why?

## Evidence
- Ponzi's impersonator works out of [[The Slaughterhouse]], owned by [[Jacob Dover]]. ([[I-01 Mystery of the Ponzi Potion|I-01]])
- [[Dover's Book]]: Ponzi once sold Dover's dying mother a useless cure. ([[I-01 Mystery of the Ponzi Potion|I-01]])
- Dover ambushes Arthur and demands TJ be brought to the slaughterhouse. ([[I-04 TJs and Js - Suspects of Devilwork|I-04]])
- Demons appear; Dover performs a moonless summoning ([[Dover's Ritual]]). ([[I-06 The Cliffs of Dover|I-06]])

## Where it stands
TJ killed Dover mid-ritual and the slaughterhouse burned (I-06). The journal never says in so many words that Dover *was* the impersonator.
