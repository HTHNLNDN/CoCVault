# What the Sleepers Want

> [!question] The question
> What are the Sleepers, and what does the Dreamer want?

## Evidence
- TJ links the Dreamer realm to God. ([[I-05 Cliffs before Dover|I-05]])
- The old man: "Go west to find the East." ([[I-08 It's Just Jimmy|I-08]])
- "The sleeper will wake. The world will sleep." ([[I-25 Dreamers Abound|I-25]])
- A German spellbook on interpreting the Dreamer's messages. ([[II-12 The Forbidden Library|II-12]])
- Dreams reach mostly creative people, in outbreaks tied to a place. ([[II-14 The Gospels of the Dreamer|II-14]])
- **The Dreamer is [[Cthulhu]]**, who created the [[Esoteric Order of Dagon]] while sleeping. ([[II-16 The Cult of Cthulhu|II-16]])
- To stop Cthulhu rising, "find what bound the Dreamers". ([[II-17 The Prophecy of the Stones|II-17]])

## Where it stands
The Dreamer is identified: **Cthulhu**. Still unknown: who or what "the Sleepers" coming for the old man are, and what bound the Dreamers.
