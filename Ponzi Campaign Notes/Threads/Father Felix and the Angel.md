# Father Felix and the Angel

> [!question] The question
> What was the "angel" Father Felix served — and is it still out there?

## Evidence
- Felix claims an angel visited him; undying flame in his basement. ([[II-04 The Search for Ponzi|II-04]])
- He needed a congregation "to contain the angel"; he exploded into wax. ([[II-05 Father Felix|II-05]])
- His notes: preserve "the herald"; instructions to consume an angel. ([[II-07 The Bible and the Everliving Flame|II-07]])

## Where it stands
Felix is dead and his flame is at the Blackwood Estate. The angel itself is unaccounted for.
