# Fungal Humans

> [!abstract] 🐙 Mythos
> A colony of small fungus resembling humans, seen at Shelly's. Compare the fungal growth on Long-Armed John.

## What we know
- Seen through a window in section C. ([[II-02 The Jersey Devil|II-02]])
- John's corpse covered in slimy fungal growth. ([[II-06 Chief Luther and Long-Armed John|II-06]])
