# Fish People

> [!abstract] 🐙 Mythos
> Innsmouth folk with bulging eyes and high foreheads — and bodies "much more fish than human".

- Innsmouth locals, distrustful. ([[I-23 In Insmuth|I-23]])
- One drops through the car roof. ([[I-25 Dreamers Abound|I-25]])
