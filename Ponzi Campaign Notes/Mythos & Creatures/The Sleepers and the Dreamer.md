# The Sleepers and the Dreamer

![[a18-sleeper-will-wake.jpg|220]]

> [!abstract] 🐙 Mythos
> Recurring name for something that sleeps and dreams: the old man waits for "the Sleepers", TJ ties "the Dreamer realm" to God, and "The sleeper will wake. The world will sleep."

## What we know
- TJ thinks the Dreamer realm is related to God. ([[I-05 Cliffs before Dover|I-05]])
- The old man awaits the Sleepers: "Go west to find the East." ([[I-08 It's Just Jimmy|I-08]])
- "The sleeper will wake. The world will sleep." / THE END IS NIGH. ([[I-25 Dreamers Abound|I-25]])
- A German spellbook on interpreting the Dreamer's messages. ([[II-12 The Forbidden Library|II-12]])
