# The Angel

> [!abstract] 🐙 Mythos
> The being that "descended to earth" and visited Father Felix; he needed a congregation to contain it, and wrote of preserving "the herald".

## What we know
- Felix claims an angel visited him. ([[II-04 The Search for Ponzi|II-04]])
- A congregation to contain the angel. ([[II-05 Father Felix|II-05]])
- Instructions on how to consume one of the angels. ([[II-07 The Bible and the Everliving Flame|II-07]])
