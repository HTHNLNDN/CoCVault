# Deep Ones

> [!abstract] 🐙 Mythos
> An order of beings serving [[Cthulhu]] through the [[Esoteric Order of Dagon]]. Adaptive, but unable to raise[?] children themselves. Many "under-water" cities pray to Dagon.

## What we know
- The Esoteric Order of Dagon is "an order of Deep Ones". ([[II-16 The Cult of Cthulhu|II-16]])
- Weakness/strength: adaptive, but cannot themselves raise[?] kids. ([[II-16 The Cult of Cthulhu|II-16]])
- Many under-water cities pray to Dagon. ([[II-17 The Prophecy of the Stones|II-17]])
