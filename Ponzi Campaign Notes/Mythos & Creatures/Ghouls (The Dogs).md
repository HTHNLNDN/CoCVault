# Ghouls (The Dogs)

![[a14-five-clawed-beast.jpg|300]]

> [!abstract] 🐙 Mythos
> Dog-faced carrion eaters — "the Dogs". The Bureau tracks nests (Arkham Nest B) and is sedating them via embalming fluid. Hopkins is one; a group lives under the Blackwood Estate and now in Gloucester.

## What we know
- Hopkins: 2 m tall, herd-dog face, stretched suit. ([[I-21 Skulls 4 the Skull Throne|I-21]])
- Ponzi warns about "the damn dogs"; nobody believes him. Memo: Arkham Ghoul Nest B, Operation Last Supper. ([[I-22 Dog Water|I-22]])
- Grave robbers reported. ([[II-06 Chief Luther and Long-Armed John|II-06]])
- In Sebastian's basement; the narrator wants one as a trophy. ([[II-07 The Bible and the Everliving Flame|II-07]])
- Led by Anders into the Gloucester caves. ([[II-10 John, Shelly and Gloucester|II-10]])
- The dogs have left the Blackwood Estate. ([[II-14 The Gospels of the Dreamer|II-14]])
