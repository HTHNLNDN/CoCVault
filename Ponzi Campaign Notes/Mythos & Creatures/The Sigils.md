# The Sigils

![[a09-sigil-heading.jpg|260]] ![[a11-tally-glyph.jpg|120]]

> [!abstract] 🐙 Mythos
> Recurring occult symbols: Dover draws them, the old man draws them, Ponzi wakes up knowing them (and may have them tattooed on his head), and a sigil ring enchants weapons.

## What we know
- Dover draws sigils to summon. ([[I-06 The Cliffs of Dover|I-06]])
- The old man's drawings of people and sigils. ([[I-08 It's Just Jimmy|I-08]])
- Halley's-comet vision. ([[I-12 The Liar, the Snitch and the Wardrobe|I-12]])
- "When I woke I knew the sigils." ([[I-13 The Sigils|I-13]])
- Tattoos on Ponzi's head. ([[I-18 Muddy Thoughts|I-18]])
- A ring with the sigils. ([[II-07 The Bible and the Everliving Flame|II-07]])
