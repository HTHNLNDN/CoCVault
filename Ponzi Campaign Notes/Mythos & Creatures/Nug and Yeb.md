# Nug and Yeb

> [!abstract] 🐙 Mythos
> Children of Yog-Sothoth; they represent life and creation, "dancing with each other in all eternity until the time is right". Named on the 5th gate.

## What we know
- "Nug & Yeb: Seek light, Birth, L:" — the 5th gate. ([[II-15 The Gates and the Children|II-15]])
- Children of Yog-Sothoth. Nug → Cthulhu; Yeb → Sathshohau[?]. "Children of the gods". ([[II-15 The Gates and the Children|II-15]])

## Connections
- [[Cthulhu]]
- [[The Gates on the Stones]]

> [!tip] Mythos lore
> In the published mythos, Nug fathered Cthulhu and Yeb fathered **Tsathoggua**, which is probably the name read as "Sathshohau".
