# The Dragon (Byakhee)

![[a11-baby-dragon.jpg|200]] ![[a12-marsh-wizard-handout.jpg|240]]

> [!abstract] 🐙 Mythos
> Winged "dragon" hatched from a pearlescent stone in Corpus Christi; Ponzi names it a **Byakhee**. Folklore tells of a wizard riding such a beast.

## What we know
- Hatched by Albert. ([[I-16 Mary Marry Maybe|I-16]])
- A sighting 20 years earlier had a man riding it. ([[I-17 Eggs of Power|I-17]])
- "Banished"; Ponzi thinks he could control it. ([[I-18 Muddy Thoughts|I-18]])
- Byakhee tracks on the island. ([[I-19 Prismatic Shards|I-19]])
