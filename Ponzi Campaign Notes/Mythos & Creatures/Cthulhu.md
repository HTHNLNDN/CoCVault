# Cthulhu

> [!abstract] 🐙 Mythos
> **The Dreamer.** High priest of Asoteth[?], leader of the [[Star Spawn]], who came from the stars. While sleeping he created the [[Esoteric Order of Dagon]]. The stones prophesy that he will rise.

## What we know
- "I found the Dreamer, Cthulhu." ([[II-16 The Cult of Cthulhu|II-16]])
- Nug is his parent among the Children of the gods ([[Nug and Yeb]]). ([[II-15 The Gates and the Children|II-15]])
- Came from the stars with the Star Spawn; created a cult while sleeping. ([[II-16 The Cult of Cthulhu|II-16]])
- The Innsmouth stones: "Cthulhu will rise. When the distant lights align…" ([[II-17 The Prophecy of the Stones|II-17]])
