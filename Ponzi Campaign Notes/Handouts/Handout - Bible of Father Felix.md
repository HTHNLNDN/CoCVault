## Left page — *Beatissimus Luke* / Introduction
> *Incipit liber primus de naturae mysteriis et divinis revelationibus, scriptum anno Domini MCLXII per manus Fratris Adalberti in monasterio Sancti Augustini.*
> ⟦He spends a bit of time contemplating his own relation to the original disciples. You can feel the zeal in his notes, but as the book continues they get more opinionated dismissing much of the text as misinterpretations, or outright fabrications.⟧
> *Primum omnium, de motu sphaerarum caelestium dicendum est…*
> He commanded my preparation, for the labor is vast and the hourglass drains. First, I must preserve the herald; he braved the abyss to deliver this word, and a vessel must be forged for his grace. Though a grand silence has fallen since our communion, the night grants me visions of the path, and when I blind myself with the sacred light, it steers my soul.
> ⟦For convenience, any text in double square brackets (shown here as ⟦ ⟧) are out-of-character notes or summaries. The Latin text is from the original book he annotated (unless it's in the margins, which are his ramblings), the English text is Oakley's notes.⟧
> *De elementorum naturis quoque tractandum est…*
> ⟦He needed a meltable medium, for the ritual that transformed him. He choose wax from alter candles⟧
> The sanctified wax is the conduit, it dissolves the physical world to reveal the divine. In it I will bathe, for the flames are not my executioner, they are my chariot. I can already feel the holy spark in my chest waiting to consume this fragile flesh and lift me to the heavens.
> *Sed haec omnia ad maiorem Dei gloriam scripta.*
> Margins: *"Ianuarius MCMXX"* (January 1920) · *"Dolor dignus erit"* · *"Non satis non satis"*

## Right page — Enchantments / Prayers
> **Enchantments** — The divine grace has shielded me and bestowed gifts far beyond wildest dreams. Yet I am but a single instrument in His grand design. I must gather a congregation, and for that, a holy sanctuary must be raised. Such a monument demands the tribute of this world's wealth. Fortuitously, the very blessings that elevate me have provided the means to harvest it.
> ⟦All enchantments require the wax or a similar substitute and the ethereal flame. To create more wax at least part of the ritual Oakley completed on himself is required, though it does not require the caster to become a vessel. In addition a focus is required, unless the caster is a vessel, and the sun must be up.⟧
> **Focus: Prayer Beads** — ⟦Diagrams meticulously describe how to craft a set beads, he notes their importance in the prayers. The runes themselves do not look particular Christian, but if Oakley noticed he didn't comment on it. Crafting this require some object for the runes, 2 hours to inscribe them, some of the wax and POW the object then carries.⟧
> **Investment of Fire** — ⟦The exact cost and effect depends on the item. In general the item must be a container, and will infuse its content with fire. Using the item will cost something, but the higher the upfront cost, the lower the cost per use.⟧
> **Prayers** — ⟦Exact costs will be fully known on first successful casting.⟧ ⟦Most of the prayers have a latin component, and sometimes a real, albeit uncommon, prayer. Their effects are not as the miracles mentioned in the bible and you question their actual origin.⟧
> **Spell: Purifying Flame** — As apostles of the Holy Father before me, I've been granted his healing touch. By his divine light the corruption shall be burned from flesh and soul of the afflicted. The worthy shall persevere! ⟦Requires a focus, 24 hours (sunrise to sunrise), a long chant, and helpers to maintain the burning of a special incense made from Frankincense and Myrrh bathed in the wax, that must be burning the entire time. A rather violent spell that has a cost for both target and caster.⟧
> **Spell: Invocatio Solis Invicti** — ⟦Requires a focus, and clear skies above the target. The prayer is short, but the effect is delayed by minutes. When it activates, a ray of light follows the target, burning it and everything near it for a duration that depends on the MP invested.⟧
> *Splendor paternae gloriae, de luce lucem proferens, Lux lucis et fons luminis, diem dies illuminans. Veni, Sol Iustitiae, et illumina tenebras nostras… Fiat lux quae urat, fiat lux quae vincat! In lumine tuo videbimus lumen, et in splendore tuo conteremus tenebras. Amen.*
> **Spell: Ardens Rancor** — ⟦A channeled spell. Requires the focus and a source of fire. The longer it is channeled the more damage and range. Can use up X MP, where X is up to 6. Casting time: X rounds. Range is X times 15 yards. The spell is homing, but moving targets may attempt a HARD dodge roll. LUCK determines if the target is lit on fire.⟧
> Margin: *"Utere regula quattuor centesimarum…"* ("use the four-percent rule…")
